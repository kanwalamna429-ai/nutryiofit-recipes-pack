Gluten Free Savory Breakfast Oatmeal Bowl — a healthy halal recipe with 380 calories and 16g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup gluten-free rolled oats
  • 2 cups vegetable broth or water
  • 2 eggs, poached or fried
  • ½ avocado, sliced
  • ½ cup sautéed mushrooms and spinach
  • 2 tbsp feta or nutritional yeast
  • Salt, pepper, red pepper flakes, fresh herbs
Instructions
  1. 1 Cook oats in broth until creamy, about 5-7 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
  2. 2 Divide into bowls. Top with cooked eggs, avocado, sautéed veggies, and cheese.
  3. 3 Sprinkle herbs and red pepper flakes.
