Creamy Mushroom Pasta (Without Cream) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 420 calories and 16g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 12 oz pasta
  • 12 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 onion, 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 cup vegetable broth
  • ½ cup cashews (soaked) or white beans blended smooth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, thyme, salt, pepper, nutritional yeast or parmesan
  • Fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Cook pasta. Blend soaked cashews with broth into creamy sauce.
  2. 2Sauté onion, garlic, and mushrooms until golden. Add thyme and blended sauce; simmer until thickened.
  3. 3Toss with cooked pasta. Add pasta water if needed. Garnish with parsley and nutritional yeast.
