Low Carb Spinach Artichoke Egg Casserole — a healthy halal recipe with 230 calories and 15g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 8 large eggs
  • 2 cups fresh spinach, chopped
  • 1 cup canned artichoke hearts, drained and chopped
  • ½ cup shredded mozzarella or feta
  • ½ cup cream cheese, softened
  • ½ cup milk
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, red pepper flakes
  • Fresh parsley for garnish
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease a 9x9 baking dish.
  2. 2 Sauté spinach and garlic in olive oil until wilted. Stir in chopped artichokes.
  3. 3 Whisk eggs, milk, cream cheese, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes. Mix in veggies and half the shredded cheese.
  4. 4 Pour into dish, top with remaining cheese. Bake 35-40 minutes until center is set. Garnish with parsley.
