Air Fryer Okra (Crispy Not Slimy) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 110 calories and 4g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb fresh okra, sliced or whole
  • 2 tbsp chickpea flour or cornstarch
  • 1 tsp each cumin, turmeric, chili powder
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, lemon wedges
Instructions
  1. 1Toss okra with flour and dry spices. Drizzle with oil and mix.
  2. 2Air fry at 400°F (205°C) for 10-14 minutes, shaking often, until crispy.
  3. 3Squeeze lemon over top and serve hot as side or snack.
