Anti Inflammatory Turmeric Chicken Skewers — a healthy halal recipe with 360 kcal calories and 38g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 lbs halal chicken thighs, cut into chunks
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp fresh turmeric, grated (or 1 tbsp powder)
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • Juice of 2 lemons
  • 1 tsp cumin and black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • Wooden skewers (soaked)
Instructions
  1. 1Mix oil, turmeric, ginger, garlic, lemon juice, and spices. Marinate chicken for 30+ minutes.
  2. 2Thread chicken onto skewers.
  3. 3Grill or broil 6-8 minutes per side until cooked to 165°F. Serve with extra lemon.
