Creamy Banana Peanut Butter Oat Smoothie — a healthy halal recipe with 480 calories and 15g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 2 tbsp natural peanut butter
  • ½ cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
Instructions
  1. 1 Add banana, peanut butter, oats, almond milk, cinnamon, and chia seeds to a blender.
  2. 2 Blend until thick and creamy (30–45 seconds).
  3. 3 Pour and enjoy as a filling breakfast smoothie.
