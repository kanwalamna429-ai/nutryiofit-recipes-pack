Moroccan Harira Vegetarian Version — a healthy halal recipe with 270 calories and 13g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped; 1 carrot, diced; 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1.5 tsp cumin, 1 tsp turmeric, ½ tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp ginger, ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
  • ¾ cup lentils, rinsed
  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
  • 6-7 cups vegetable broth
  • ¼ cup each fresh cilantro and parsley, chopped
  • Lemon wedges for serving
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion, carrot, celery 7 min. Add garlic and spices 2 min.
  2. 2Add tomatoes; cook 5 min. Stir in lentils, chickpeas, and broth.
  3. 3Simmer 40-45 min until lentils are soft. Stir in herbs. Serve with lemon.
