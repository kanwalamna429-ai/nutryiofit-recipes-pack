One Pot Red Lentil Tomato Turmeric Soup — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 240 calories and 13g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1 (28 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp chili powder
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 (14 oz) can coconut milk or 1 cup plain yogurt (stirred in at end)
  • Lemon juice, cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion, garlic, ginger. Add spices and tomatoes; cook 3 minutes.
  2. 2Add lentils and broth. Simmer 20 minutes until lentils dissolve.
  3. 3Stir in coconut milk or yogurt. Blend if smoother texture desired. Finish with lemon and herbs.
