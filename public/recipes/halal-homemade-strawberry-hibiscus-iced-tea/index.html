Homemade Strawberry Hibiscus Iced Tea — a healthy halal recipe with 35 calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 hibiscus tea bags (or ¼ cup dried hibiscus flowers)
  • 2 cups fresh strawberries, hulled
  • 4 cups boiling water
  • 1–2 tbsp honey or monk fruit (optional)
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Ice and fresh mint
Instructions
  1. 1 Steep hibiscus in boiling water for 8–10 minutes. Remove bags and cool.
  2. 2 Blend strawberries with a little tea until smooth; strain if desired.
  3. 3 Mix strawberry puree, hibiscus tea, lemon juice, and sweetener. Chill.
  4. 4 Serve over ice.
