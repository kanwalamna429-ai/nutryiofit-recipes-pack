Diabetic Friendly Vegetable Chicken Soup — a healthy halal recipe with 240 kcal calories and 28g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs halal chicken thighs, diced
  • 6 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 4 cups mixed low-carb vegetables (zucchini, broccoli, celery, spinach)
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried thyme and black pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Heat oil in pot. Sauté onion, garlic, and chicken until lightly browned.
  2. 2Add broth, hearty vegetables, thyme, and pepper. Simmer 25-30 minutes.
  3. 3Stir in spinach last 5 minutes. Garnish with parsley.
