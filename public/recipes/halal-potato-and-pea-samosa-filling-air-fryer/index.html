Potato and Pea Samosa Filling (Air Fryer) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 240 calories and 7g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1 cup green peas
  • 1 onion, 1 green chili
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, cumin seeds, turmeric, garam masala, coriander powder
  • 2 tbsp oil, salt, cilantro, lemon juice
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté cumin seeds, onion, chili, and ginger-garlic. Add peas and spices.
  2. 2Mix in mashed potatoes and cook 5 minutes. Finish with cilantro and lemon. Cool completely.
  3. 3Use in samosa pastry and air fry at 380°F for 12-15 minutes until golden, or bake.
