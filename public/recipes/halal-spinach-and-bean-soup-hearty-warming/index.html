Spinach and Bean Soup (Hearty Warming) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 240 calories and 13g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 8 cups fresh spinach
  • 2 (15 oz) cans white beans or cannellini, drained
  • 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves, 2 carrots
  • 1 (14 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 5 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp thyme, salt, pepper, lemon juice
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion, carrot, and garlic in olive oil. Add tomatoes and thyme; cook 5 minutes.
  2. 2Stir in beans and broth. Simmer 15 minutes.
  3. 3Add spinach and cook until wilted. Finish with lemon juice and black pepper.
