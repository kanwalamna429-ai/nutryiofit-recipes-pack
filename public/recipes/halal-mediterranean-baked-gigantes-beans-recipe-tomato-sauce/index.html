Mediterranean Baked Gigantes Beans Recipe Tomato Sauce — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 290 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 (15 oz) cans butter beans (gigantes) or large white beans, drained
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp each: dried oregano, cinnamon (optional), paprika
  • Fresh dill and parsley
  • Salt and black pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 375°F. Heat olive oil in an oven-safe skillet or pot. Sauté onion until soft. Add garlic and tomato paste.
  2. 2Stir in crushed tomatoes, spices, salt, and pepper. Simmer 5 minutes.
  3. 3Add beans and mix well. Transfer to oven (or cover and bake).
  4. 4Bake 40-45 minutes until sauce thickens. Garnish with fresh herbs and extra olive oil.
