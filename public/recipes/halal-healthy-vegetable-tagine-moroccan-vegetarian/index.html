Healthy Vegetable Tagine (Moroccan Vegetarian) — a healthy halal recipe with 300 calories and 10g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves, 1-inch ginger
  • 2 tsp ras el hanout or (cumin + coriander + cinnamon + paprika)
  • 2 carrots, 1 sweet potato, 1 zucchini, 1 bell pepper, 1 eggplant (chunked)
  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas
  • 1 (15 oz) can diced tomatoes + 1-2 cups veg broth
  • Handful dried apricots or raisins (optional), fresh cilantro, lemon
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion, garlic, ginger, spices. Add veggies and cook 5 min.
  2. 2Add tomatoes, broth, chickpeas, and dried fruit. Simmer covered 25-30 min until tender.
  3. 3Finish with lemon and herbs. Serve with couscous.
