Lebanese Cheese and Za'atar Manakish - Homemade — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 380 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • Dough: 3 cups flour, 1 tsp yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1 tsp salt, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 cup warm water
Instructions
  1. 1Knead dough ingredients until smooth. Let rise 30-45 min.
  2. 2Divide into 6 balls. Roll into thin circles.
  3. 3Mix za'atar with olive oil into paste. Spread on dough, top with cheese.
  4. 4Bake at 450°F (230°C) on a hot baking sheet or pizza stone for 8-12 min until golden and cheese melts.
