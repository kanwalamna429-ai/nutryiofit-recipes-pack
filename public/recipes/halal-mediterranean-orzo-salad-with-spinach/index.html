Mediterranean Orzo Salad with Spinach

Mediterranean Orzo Salad with Spinach — a healthy halal recipe with 260 calories and 7g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup orzo pasta
  • 4 cups fresh baby spinach, chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ cucumber, diced
  • ½ red onion, diced
  • ½ cup Kalamata olives, halved
  • ¼ cup fresh basil and oregano
  • Dressing: ¼ cup olive oil, juice of 1 lemon, 1 tbsp apple vinegar, 1 garlic clove, salt & pepper.
Instructions
  1. 1Cook orzo al dente, drain, and rinse under cold water.
  2. 2Whisk dressing. Combine orzo with spinach, vegetables, olives, and herbs.
  3. 3Toss with dressing. Add optional halal feta if desired.
