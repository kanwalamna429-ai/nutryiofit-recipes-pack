Low Carb Zucchini Noodle Shakshuka Recipe — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 220 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium zucchini, spiralized
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp each: cumin, paprika, chili flakes
  • 4-6 large eggs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Fresh cilantro or parsley
  • Feta for topping (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion and bell pepper in oil. Add garlic and spices.
  2. 2Pour in tomatoes; simmer 10 minutes. Add zucchini noodles; cook 3-4 minutes.
  3. 3Make wells and crack eggs into sauce. Cover and cook until eggs set (5-7 min).
  4. 4Garnish with herbs and feta.
