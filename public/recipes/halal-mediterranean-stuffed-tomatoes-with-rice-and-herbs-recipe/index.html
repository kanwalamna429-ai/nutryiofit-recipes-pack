Mediterranean Stuffed Tomatoes with Rice and Herbs Recipe — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 280 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 8 large ripe tomatoes
  • 1 cup cooked rice (or quinoa)
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ cup pine nuts or walnuts (optional)
  • ½ cup fresh parsley, mint, and dill, chopped
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Cut tops off tomatoes and scoop out pulp (reserve).
  2. 2Sauté onion and garlic in olive oil. Mix with rice, herbs, chopped tomato pulp, pine nuts, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  3. 3Stuff tomatoes and place in baking dish. Drizzle with olive oil.
  4. 4Bake covered at 375°F for 30 minutes, then uncovered 10-15 minutes.
