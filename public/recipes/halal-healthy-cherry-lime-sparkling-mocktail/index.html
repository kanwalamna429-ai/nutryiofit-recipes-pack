Healthy Cherry Lime Sparkling Mocktail — a healthy halal recipe with 60 calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup pure cherry juice (no added sugar) or 1½ cups fresh pitted cherries
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 cup sparkling water
  • 1 tbsp monk fruit sweetener (optional)
  • Ice cubes
  • Lime wedges and fresh cherries for garnish
Instructions
  1. 1 If using fresh cherries, blend them with a splash of water and strain.
  2. 2 In a pitcher, mix cherry juice/puree, lime juice, and sweetener.
  3. 3 Add ice and top with sparkling water just before serving. Stir gently.
  4. 4 Garnish with lime wedges and cherries.
