Healthy Zucchini Lemon Soup — a healthy halal recipe with 120 calories and 4g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 lbs zucchini, chopped
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • Zest and juice of 2 lemons
  • ½ cup coconut milk (optional)
  • Fresh dill or parsley, salt & pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion 5 min. Add garlic and zucchini 4 min.
  2. 2Add broth and simmer 20 min until tender.
  3. 3Blend smooth. Stir in lemon zest, juice, and coconut milk if using. Garnish with herbs.
