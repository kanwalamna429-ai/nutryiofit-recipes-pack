Healthy Moroccan Couscous Vegetable Bowl — a healthy halal recipe with 380 calories and 13g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup couscous
  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas
  • 2 carrots, 1 zucchini, 1 bell pepper, 1 red onion (chopped)
  • 2 tsp ras el hanout or Moroccan spice mix
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, 2 tbsp raisins, fresh cilantro and mint
  • Lemon wedges, toasted almonds
Instructions
  1. 1Cook couscous according to package. Fluff and set aside.
  2. 2Roast or sauté vegetables with spices and chickpeas until tender (15-20 min).
  3. 3Mix couscous with vegetables, raisins, herbs, and a drizzle of oil.
  4. 4Top with almonds and serve with lemon.
