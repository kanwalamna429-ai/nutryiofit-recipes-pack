Creamy Roasted Tomato Basil Soup (Vegetarian) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 220 calories and 4g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 3 lbs ripe tomatoes, halved
  • 1 large onion, quartered
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • ½ cup heavy cream or coconut cream
  • Salt, pepper, sugar (pinch)
Instructions
  1. 1Roast tomatoes, onion, and garlic with olive oil, salt, and pepper at 425°F for 30–35 min.
  2. 2Blend roasted vegetables with basil and broth until smooth.
  3. 3Simmer 5–10 min. Stir in cream and adjust seasoning.
  4. 4Serve with grilled cheese or croutons.
