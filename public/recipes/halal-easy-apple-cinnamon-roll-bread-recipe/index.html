Easy Apple Cinnamon Roll Bread — a healthy halal recipe with 170 kcal calories and 4g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups white whole wheat flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda
  • 2 tsp cinnamon (divided)
  • Pinch salt
  • 2 eggs
  • ¾ cup unsweetened applesauce
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup finely diced apples
  • 2 tbsp coconut sugar for swirl
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease loaf pan.
  2. 2Mix flour, baking powder, soda, 1 tsp cinnamon, and salt.
  3. 3Whisk eggs, applesauce, maple, oil, and vanilla. Stir in apples.
  4. 4Combine wet and dry. Pour half batter in pan, sprinkle cinnamon-coconut sugar, add remaining batter and swirl.
  5. 5Bake 45-50 minutes. Cool fully.
