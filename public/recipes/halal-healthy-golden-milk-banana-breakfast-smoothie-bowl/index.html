Healthy Golden Milk Banana Breakfast Smoothie Bowl — a healthy halal recipe with 280 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 frozen bananas
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • ½ tsp ground ginger and cinnamon
  • Pinch of black pepper
  • 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • Toppings: banana slices, almonds, coconut flakes, chia seeds
Instructions
  1. 1 Blend frozen bananas, milk, yogurt, turmeric, ginger, cinnamon, pepper, and sweetener until thick and smooth.
  2. 2 Pour into bowls.
  3. 3 Arrange toppings generously.
  4. 4 Serve immediately.
