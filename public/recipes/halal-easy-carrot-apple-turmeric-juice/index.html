Easy Carrot Apple Turmeric Juice Recipe

Easy Carrot Apple Turmeric Juice — a healthy halal recipe with 130 calories and 2g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium carrots, peeled
  • 2 apples, cored
  • ½ tsp ground turmeric
  • Pinch black pepper
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • ½ cup cold water
Instructions
  1. 1 Juice carrots and apples using a juicer.
  2. 2 Stir in turmeric, black pepper, lemon juice, and water.
  3. 3 Serve fresh over ice if desired.
