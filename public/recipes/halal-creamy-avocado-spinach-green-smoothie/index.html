Creamy Avocado Spinach Green Smoothie — a healthy halal beverage with 350 calories and 15g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • ½ ripe avocado
  • 2 cups fresh spinach
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt or coconut yogurt
  • Juice of ½ lime
  • Ice if needed
Instructions
  1. 1Scoop avocado and add all ingredients to blender.
  2. 2Blend until ultra-creamy.
  3. 3Enjoy as a nutrient-dense meal replacement.
