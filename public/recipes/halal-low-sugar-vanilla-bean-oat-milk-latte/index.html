Low Sugar Vanilla Bean Oat Milk Latte — a healthy halal beverage with 150 calories and 3g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup unsweetened oat milk
  • ½ cup strong brewed coffee or espresso (cooled for iced)
  • ½ tsp vanilla bean paste or pure vanilla extract
  • ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp maple syrup (optional, for low sugar)
  • Ice cubes (for iced version)
Instructions
  1. 1Gently warm oat milk with vanilla and cinnamon. Froth or whisk until foamy.
  2. 2Pour coffee into a mug (hot) or glass with ice (iced).
  3. 3Top with frothed oat milk and a light sprinkle of cinnamon.
