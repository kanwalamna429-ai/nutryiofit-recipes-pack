One Pot Chana Saag (Spinach Chickpeas) — a healthy halal recipe with 320 calories and 15g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained
  • 1 large onion, 4 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp ginger
  • 1 (10-12 oz) bag spinach, chopped
  • 1 (15 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp turmeric, chili powder
  • ½ cup coconut milk or yogurt, 1 tbsp oil, salt
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion in oil until golden. Add garlic, ginger, and spices; cook 1 min.
  2. 2Stir in tomatoes and chickpeas. Simmer 10 min.
  3. 3Add spinach and coconut milk. Cook 5-8 min until spinach wilts and sauce thickens. Serve with rice or naan.
