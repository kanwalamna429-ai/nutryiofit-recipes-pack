One Pot Green Lentil Vegetable Curry (Mild) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 260 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup green lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 2 potatoes, cubed
  • 1 zucchini or spinach (added later)
  • 1 (14 oz) can coconut milk or diced tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp mild curry powder, 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp oil, salt, fresh cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion in oil. Add ginger-garlic and spices; cook 1 minute.
  2. 2Add lentils, carrots, potatoes, and 3 cups water. Simmer 25-30 minutes until lentils are soft.
  3. 3Stir in coconut milk or tomatoes and zucchini/spinach. Simmer 5-10 minutes. Garnish and serve with rice.
