High Protein Falafel Protein Bowl Recipe with Tahini — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 480 calories and 20g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • Falafel:
  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • ½ cup fresh parsley and cilantro
  • 2 tsp ground cumin and coriander
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 3-4 tbsp chickpea flour
  • Salt, pepper, olive oil spray
Instructions
  1. 1Blend falafel ingredients (except flour) into coarse paste. Mix in flour. Form balls or patties.
  2. 2Bake at 400°F on lined sheet, sprayed with oil, 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway.
  3. 3Assemble bowls: quinoa base, veggies, falafel, generous tahini drizzle.
