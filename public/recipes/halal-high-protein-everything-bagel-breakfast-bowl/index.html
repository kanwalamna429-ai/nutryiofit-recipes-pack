High Protein Everything Bagel Breakfast Bowl — a healthy halal recipe with 450 calories and 28g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 eggs or 8 egg whites, scrambled
  • 1 cup cooked quinoa or cauliflower rice
  • ½ avocado, sliced
  • ½ cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp cream cheese or Greek yogurt
  • 1-2 tsp everything bagel seasoning
  • 4 oz smoked salmon or turkey slices (halal)
  • Fresh dill or chives
Instructions
  1. 1 Scramble eggs with seasoning.
  2. 2 Warm quinoa/rice base in bowls. Top with cream cheese/yogurt, eggs, avocado, tomatoes, protein, and extra seasoning.
  3. 3 Garnish with herbs.
