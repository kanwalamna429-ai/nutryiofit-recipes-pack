Dairy Free Shrimp Coconut Curry — a healthy halal recipe with 380 kcal calories and 28g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs halal large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 (13.5 oz) can full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp coconut oil
  • Salt, pepper, and fresh cilantro
  • Lime wedges
Instructions
  1. 1Heat coconut oil. Sauté onion, garlic, and ginger until soft. Add spices and cook 1 minute.
  2. 2Pour in coconut milk and simmer 5 minutes.
  3. 3Add shrimp and cook 4-6 minutes until pink and curled. Garnish with cilantro and lime. Serve with cauliflower rice.
