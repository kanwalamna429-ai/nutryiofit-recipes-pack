Roasted Carrot Turmeric Soup (Healthy) — a healthy halal recipe with 160 calories and 3g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 lbs carrots, chopped
  • 1 onion, quartered; 4 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp turmeric, ½ tsp cumin, ½ tsp ginger powder
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • ½ cup coconut milk
  • Salt & pepper, fresh cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Toss carrots, onion, and garlic with oil and spices. Roast at 425°F for 35-40 min.
  2. 2Blend roasted vegetables with broth until smooth.
  3. 3Simmer 10 min. Stir in coconut milk and adjust seasoning. Garnish with cilantro.
