Pan-Fried Paneer with Spiced Tomato Sauce — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 310 calories and 17g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 14 oz paneer, sliced or cubed
  • 3 large tomatoes, finely chopped or pureed
  • 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ginger paste
  • 1 tsp cumin, turmeric, garam masala, Kashmiri chili powder
  • 2 tbsp oil, salt, fresh cilantro, kasuri methi (dried fenugreek) optional
Instructions
  1. 1Pan-fry paneer in 1 tbsp oil until golden on both sides; remove and set aside.
  2. 2In the same pan, sauté onion, garlic, and ginger. Add spices and tomatoes; cook 10-12 minutes until thick sauce forms.
  3. 3Return paneer to the sauce and simmer 3-4 minutes. Crush kasuri methi between palms and add. Garnish with cilantro.
