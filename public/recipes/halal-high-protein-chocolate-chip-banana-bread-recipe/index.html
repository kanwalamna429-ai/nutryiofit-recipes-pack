High Protein Chocolate Chip Banana Bread — a healthy halal recipe with 215 kcal calories and 11g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1½ cups whole wheat flour
  • ½ cup vanilla protein powder (halal)
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp cinnamon, pinch salt
  • 2 eggs
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • ½ cup halal dark chocolate chips
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease loaf pan.
  2. 2Whisk flour, protein powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt.
  3. 3Mix bananas, eggs, maple, oil, yogurt, and vanilla.
  4. 4Combine mixtures. Fold in chocolate chips.
  5. 5Bake 50-60 minutes. Cool fully before slicing.
