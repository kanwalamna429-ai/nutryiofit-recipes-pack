Baked Cherry Almond Protein Breakfast Bars — a healthy halal recipe with 280 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • ½ cup almond butter
  • ⅓ cup honey or maple syrup
  • 2 scoops vanilla protein powder
  • 1 cup dried cherries (unsweetened)
  • ½ cup sliced almonds
  • 1 tsp almond extract and cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line 8x8 pan.
  2. 2 Mix almond butter, honey, protein powder, almond extract, cinnamon, and salt.
  3. 3 Stir in oats, cherries, and almonds until combined.
  4. 4 Press firmly into pan. Bake 22-25 minutes.
  5. 5 Cool completely before cutting into bars. Store in fridge.
