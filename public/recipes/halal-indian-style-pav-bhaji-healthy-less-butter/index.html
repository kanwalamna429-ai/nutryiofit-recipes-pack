Indian Style Pav Bhaji (Healthy Less Butter) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 380 calories and 9g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 potatoes, 1 cauliflower, ½ cup peas, 2 carrots (boiled & mashed)
  • 2 onions, 3 tomatoes, 1 bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2–3 tsp pav bhaji masala, ½ tsp turmeric, chili powder
  • 2 tbsp butter or oil, salt, lemon, fresh cilantro, pav buns
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion, ginger-garlic, tomatoes, and bell pepper. Add spices.
  2. 2Mash in boiled vegetables and simmer 10–12 min until blended.
  3. 3Toast pav buns with minimal butter.
  4. 4Serve bhaji with lemon, cilantro, and toasted pav.
