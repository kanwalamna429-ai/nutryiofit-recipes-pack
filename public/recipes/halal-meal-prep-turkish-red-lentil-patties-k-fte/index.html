Meal Prep Turkish Red Lentil Patties (Köfte) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 280 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup red lentils
  • ½ cup fine bulgur
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp Aleppo pepper
  • Fresh parsley and green onions, chopped
  • Salt, olive oil for brushing
Instructions
  1. 1Cook lentils in 3 cups water until soft and water absorbed. Stir in bulgur, cover, and let rest 10 minutes.
  2. 2Sauté onion, add tomato paste and spices. Mix into lentil-bulgur with herbs. Shape into patties.
  3. 3Bake at 400°F (205°C) for 20 minutes or pan-sear. Cool and store. Serve with salad or in wraps. Keeps 5 days.
