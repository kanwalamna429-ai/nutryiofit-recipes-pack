Low Sugar Raspberry Hibiscus Lemonade — a healthy halal recipe with 40 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 hibiscus tea bags
  • 1 cup fresh raspberries
  • Juice of 3 lemons
  • 4 cups water
  • Monk fruit or stevia to taste
  • Ice and lemon slices
Instructions
  1. 1 Steep hibiscus tea in 2 cups hot water for 5–7 minutes, then cool.
  2. 2 Blend raspberries with lemon juice and strain into tea.
  3. 3 Add remaining water and sweetener. Chill and serve over ice.
