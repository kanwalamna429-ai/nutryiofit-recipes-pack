Low Calorie Mango Sorbet Style — a healthy halal recipe with 110 kcal calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 cups frozen mango chunks
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened coconut water or almond milk
  • 1 Tbsp lime juice
  • 1-2 Tbsp monk fruit sweetener (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Add frozen mango, coconut water, lime juice, and sweetener to a high-speed blender or food processor.
  2. 2Blend until smooth and sorbet-like, adding a splash more liquid if needed.
  3. 3Serve immediately or freeze 20-30 minutes for firmer texture.
