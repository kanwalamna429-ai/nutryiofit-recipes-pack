Turkish Green Lentil and Bulgur Pilaf — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 340 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup green lentils
  • 1 cup coarse bulgur
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, salt, black pepper
  • Fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Cook lentils in 3 cups water until almost tender (15-20 min).
  2. 2Heat oil. Sauté onion until golden. Add tomato paste and spices.
  3. 3Add bulgur and cooked lentils with their liquid. Bring to boil.
  4. 4Cover and simmer 12-15 min until bulgur is tender. Rest 10 min. Fluff and garnish with parsley.
