Chickpea Flour Socca Flatbread (Easy) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 220 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup chickpea flour
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil (plus more for pan)
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Optional: black pepper, rosemary, za'atar, or garlic powder
Instructions
  1. 1Whisk chickpea flour, water, 1½ tbsp oil, and salt until smooth. Let rest 30 minutes.
  2. 2Preheat oven to 450-475°F with a 10-inch cast-iron skillet inside.
  3. 3Carefully remove hot skillet, add remaining oil, swirl, and pour in batter. Top with herbs/pepper.
  4. 4Bake 12-18 minutes until edges are crisp and golden (or broil briefly for extra color). Slice and serve warm with toppings like veggies, hummus, or salad.
