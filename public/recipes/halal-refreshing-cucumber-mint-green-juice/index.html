Refreshing Cucumber Mint Green Juice — a healthy halal recipe with 80 calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 large cucumber
  • 2 green apples, cored
  • Handful fresh mint leaves
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • ½ cup cold water or coconut water
  • Ice for serving
Instructions
  1. 1 Juice cucumber, apples, and mint using a juicer.
  2. 2 Stir in lemon juice and water.
  3. 3 Serve over ice immediately.
