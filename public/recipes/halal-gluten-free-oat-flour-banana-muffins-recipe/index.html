Gluten Free Oat Flour Banana Muffins — a healthy halal recipe with 140 kcal calories and 4g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups oat flour (certified gluten-free)
  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 2 Tbsp coconut oil or avocado oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • Pinch salt
  • Optional: Handful walnuts
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line muffin tin.
  2. 2Mix mashed bananas, eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla.
  3. 3Add dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
  4. 4Divide into muffin cups. Bake 20-25 minutes until toothpick clean.
