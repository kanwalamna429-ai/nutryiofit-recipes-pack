Chickpea and Lemon Orzo Soup — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 320 calories and 13g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup orzo pasta
  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained
  • 1 onion, diced; 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 carrots, diced; 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 6 cups vegetable broth
  • Zest and juice of 2 lemons
  • 2 cups baby spinach
  • Olive oil, salt, pepper, fresh dill or parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion, carrot, and celery in olive oil until softened. Add garlic and cook 1 minute.
  2. 2Pour in broth, bring to simmer. Add orzo and chickpeas; cook 8-10 minutes until orzo is tender.
  3. 3Stir in lemon zest, juice, and spinach until wilted. Season well and garnish with herbs.
