Paneer Do Pyaza (with Caramelized Onion) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 360 calories and 16g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 14 oz paneer, cubed
  • 3 large onions (2 thinly sliced, 1 chopped)
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin, turmeric, coriander, garam masala, red chili powder
  • 3 tbsp oil, fresh cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Caramelize sliced onions slowly until golden brown; set aside half.
  2. 2In remaining onions, add chopped onion, ginger-garlic, tomatoes, and spices. Cook until masala forms.
  3. 3Add paneer cubes and caramelized onions. Simmer 5-7 minutes. Garnish with cilantro.
