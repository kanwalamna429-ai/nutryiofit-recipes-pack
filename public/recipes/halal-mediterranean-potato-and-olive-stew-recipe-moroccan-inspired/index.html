Mediterranean Potato and Olive Stew Recipe Moroccan Inspired — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 260 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium potatoes, cubed
  • 1 cup pitted olives (mixed green and black)
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp each: cumin, paprika, ginger
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • Fresh cilantro and lemon wedges
  • Salt and pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Heat oil in a pot. Sauté onion until golden. Add garlic and spices.
  2. 2Add potatoes and tomatoes. Stir to coat. Add enough water to cover partially.
  3. 3Simmer 25-30 minutes until potatoes are tender. Stir in olives in last 10 minutes.
  4. 4Garnish with cilantro and serve with lemon wedges.
