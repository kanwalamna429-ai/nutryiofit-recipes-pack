High Protein Blueberry Cottage Cheese Muffins — a healthy halal recipe with 150 kcal calories and 13g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup cottage cheese (halal)
  • 3 eggs
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup oat flour
  • ½ cup vanilla protein powder (halal)
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1½ cups fresh or frozen blueberries
  • Pinch salt
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 375°F. Line muffin tin.
  2. 2Blend cottage cheese, eggs, yogurt, maple, and vanilla until smooth.
  3. 3Mix in oat flour, protein powder, baking powder, and salt.
  4. 4Fold in blueberries.
  5. 5Divide and bake 20-25 minutes until golden. Cool on rack.
