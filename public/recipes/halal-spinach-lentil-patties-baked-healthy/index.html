Spinach Lentil Patties (Baked Healthy) — a healthy halal recipe with 220 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup cooked green or brown lentils
  • 1 (10 oz) bag spinach, wilted and chopped
  • 1 small onion, 3 garlic cloves
  • ½ cup rolled oats or breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp ground flax + 5 tbsp water
  • 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, salt & pepper
  • Olive oil spray
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven 400°F. Mix all ingredients in a bowl until cohesive.
  2. 2Form into 12 patties and place on lined baking sheet. Spray with oil.
  3. 3Bake 22-28 min, flipping halfway until golden and firm. Serve in wraps, bowls, or with salad.
