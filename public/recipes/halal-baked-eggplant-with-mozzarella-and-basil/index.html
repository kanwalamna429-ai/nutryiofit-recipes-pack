Baked Eggplant with Mozzarella and Basil — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 320 calories and 16g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 large eggplants, sliced into ½-inch rounds
  • 8 oz fresh mozzarella, sliced
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Olive oil, salt, pepper, Italian seasoning, grated parmesan
Instructions
  1. 1Brush eggplant slices with oil, season, and bake at 425°F for 15 minutes, flipping halfway.
  2. 2Layer in a baking dish: eggplant, marinara, mozzarella, basil. Repeat. Top with parmesan.
  3. 3Bake 15-20 minutes until cheese is bubbly and golden. Rest 5 minutes before serving.
