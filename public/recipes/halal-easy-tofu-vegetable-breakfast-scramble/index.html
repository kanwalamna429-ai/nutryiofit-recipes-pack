Tofu Vegetable Breakfast Scramble — a healthy halal breakfast with 220 calories and 18g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 block firm tofu crumbled
  • ½ cup spinach
  • ¼ cup bell pepper
  • ¼ tsp turmeric
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté veggies 3 min.
  2. 2Add tofu, turmeric.
  3. 3Cook 5 min.
