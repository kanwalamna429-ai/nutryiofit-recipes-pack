Mediterranean Grilled Chicken Salad — a healthy halal recipe with 340 kcal calories and 35g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs halal boneless chicken breasts or thighs
  • 6 cups mixed greens (romaine, spinach, arugula)
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ red onion, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup Kalamata olives
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Season chicken with olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper. Grill 6-7 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 165°F. Rest and slice.
  2. 2In a large bowl, combine greens, cucumber, tomatoes, onion, and olives.
  3. 3Top with sliced grilled chicken and drizzle with extra olive oil and lemon juice. Toss gently.
