Cauliflower Rice Biryani (Vegetarian Low Carb) — a healthy halal recipe with 220 calories and 10g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 6 cups cauliflower rice (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 large onion, sliced; 3 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp ginger
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, green beans)
  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas
  • 2 tbsp biryani masala or (garam masala + turmeric + cumin)
  • 3 tbsp ghee or oil (halal), fresh cilantro and mint, fried onions (optional)
  • Lemon wedges
Instructions
  1. 1Heat ghee/oil. Sauté onion until golden. Add garlic, ginger, and spices.
  2. 2Stir in mixed vegetables and chickpeas; cook 4-5 min.
  3. 3Add cauliflower rice and cook 6-8 min until tender but not mushy.
  4. 4Garnish with herbs, fried onions, and lemon. Serve hot.
