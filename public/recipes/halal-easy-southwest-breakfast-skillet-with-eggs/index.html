Easy Southwest Breakfast Skillet with Eggs — a healthy halal recipe with 420 calories and 20g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 large sweet potato, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 1 cup black beans
  • 1 tsp chili powder, cumin, paprika, salt
  • Olive oil, salsa, avocado, cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1 Heat oil in skillet. Cook sweet potato 8-10 minutes until tender. Add onion and pepper, cook 4 minutes.
  2. 2 Stir in beans and spices.
  3. 3 Make wells and crack eggs in. Cover and cook until eggs reach desired doneness.
  4. 4 Top with salsa, avocado, and cilantro.
