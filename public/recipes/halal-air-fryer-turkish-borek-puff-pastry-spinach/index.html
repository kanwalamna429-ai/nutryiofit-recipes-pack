Air Fryer Turkish Borek (Puff Pastry Spinach) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 320 calories and 9g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 package puff pastry, thawed
  • 10 oz spinach, wilted and chopped
  • 8 oz feta cheese, crumbled
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 egg (for egg wash)
  • Salt, pepper, dill
  • Olive oil or butter spray
Instructions
  1. 1Mix spinach, feta, onion, dill, salt, and pepper.
  2. 2Cut puff pastry into squares, add filling, fold into triangles or rolls, and seal. Brush with egg wash.
  3. 3Air fry at 370°F (188°C) for 12-15 minutes until puffed and golden. Serve warm.
