Refreshing Cucumber Lime Coconut Water — a healthy halal recipe with 55 calories and 2g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 large English cucumber, chopped
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 2 cups coconut water (no added sugar)
  • 8–10 fresh mint leaves
  • Ice cubes
Instructions
  1. 1 Blend cucumber, lime juice, coconut water, and mint until smooth.
  2. 2 Strain through a fine mesh for clearer drink.
  3. 3 Serve over ice with extra mint and lime slices.
