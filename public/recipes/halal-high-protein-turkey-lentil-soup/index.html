High Protein Turkey Lentil Soup — a healthy halal recipe with 310 calories and 30g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lb ground halal turkey
  • 1 onion, diced; 2 carrots, diced; 2 celery, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup green lentils, rinsed
  • 1 (15 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 6 cups halal chicken broth
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning, salt & pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Brown turkey; remove excess fat. Add onion, carrots, celery and cook 6 min. Add garlic.
  2. 2Stir in lentils, tomatoes, broth, and seasoning.
  3. 3Simmer 35-40 min until lentils are tender.
