Low Sugar Peach Coconut Water Refresher — a healthy halal recipe with 85 calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 ripe peaches, pitted and chopped
  • 1½ cups coconut water (no added sugar)
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 6–8 fresh mint leaves
  • 1 tsp monk fruit sweetener (optional)
  • Ice cubes
Instructions
  1. 1 Blend peaches, coconut water, lime juice, mint, and sweetener until smooth.
  2. 2 Strain for clearer texture if preferred.
  3. 3 Serve over ice with mint garnish.
