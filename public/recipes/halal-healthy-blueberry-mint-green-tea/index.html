Healthy Blueberry Mint Green Tea — a healthy halal recipe with 60 calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 green tea bags
  • 1 cup fresh or frozen blueberries
  • 8–10 fresh mint leaves
  • 2 cups hot water
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp honey or monk fruit (optional)
  • Ice for iced version
Instructions
  1. 1 Steep green tea bags in hot water for 3–5 minutes. Remove bags and let cool slightly.
  2. 2 Blend blueberries and mint with a splash of tea until smooth. Strain if desired.
  3. 3 Combine blueberry mixture, remaining tea, lemon juice, and sweetener.
  4. 4 Serve warm or over ice.
