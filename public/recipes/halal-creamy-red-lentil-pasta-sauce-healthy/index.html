Creamy Red Lentil Pasta Sauce (Healthy) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 220 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cups vegetable broth or water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano, basil, red pepper flakes
  • ¼ cup nutritional yeast or grated parmesan (optional)
  • Salt and pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion and garlic in oil. Add lentils, tomatoes, broth, and herbs.
  2. 2Simmer 15-20 minutes until lentils are very soft and sauce thickens.
  3. 3Blend until smooth with immersion blender. Adjust consistency and season. Toss with cooked pasta.
