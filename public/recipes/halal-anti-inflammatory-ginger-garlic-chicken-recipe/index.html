Anti Inflammatory Ginger Garlic Chicken — a healthy halal recipe with 390 kcal calories and 36g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 lbs halal chicken thighs, bone-in or boneless
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 2 lemons
  • 1 tsp turmeric and black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Mix garlic, ginger, oil, lemon juice, turmeric, pepper, and salt. Marinate chicken 30+ minutes.
  2. 2Preheat oven to 425°F or heat skillet. Bake or pan-sear chicken 20-25 minutes until 165°F.
  3. 3Rest and garnish with cilantro.
