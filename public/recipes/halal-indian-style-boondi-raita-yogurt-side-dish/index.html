Indian Style Boondi Raita (Yogurt Side Dish) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 110 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups plain yogurt
  • 1 cup boondi (ready-made or fried gram flour pearls)
  • ½ tsp roasted cumin powder, ½ tsp chaat masala, ¼ tsp red chili powder
  • Salt, 2 tbsp chopped cilantro, 1 tbsp mint (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Whisk yogurt until smooth. Add a little water if too thick.
  2. 2Season with roasted cumin, chaat masala, chili powder, and salt.
  3. 3Add boondi and mix gently. Let sit 5 min so boondi softens slightly.
  4. 4Garnish with cilantro and mint. Serve chilled with biryani or pulao.
