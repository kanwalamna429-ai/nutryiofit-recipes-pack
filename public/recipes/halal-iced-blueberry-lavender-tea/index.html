Iced Blueberry Lavender Tea — a healthy halal recipe with 40 calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 cups water
  • 1–2 tbsp dried culinary lavender buds (food-grade)
  • 1 cup fresh or frozen blueberries
  • 2–3 tbsp honey or maple syrup (to taste, optional for low sugar)
  • 1 cup cold water or ice
  • Fresh blueberries, lemon slices, and mint for garnish
  • Ice cubes for serving
Instructions
  1. 1 Bring 4 cups water to a boil. Remove from heat and add lavender buds and blueberries. Steep for 5–10 minutes.
  2. 2 Mash blueberries gently to release flavor. Strain into a pitcher.
  3. 3 Stir in honey/maple syrup while warm. Add cold water or ice. Chill in the fridge for 1–2 hours.
  4. 4 Serve over ice with garnishes.
