Healthy Dragon Fruit Coconut Chia Pudding — a healthy halal recipe with 270 calories and 8g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • ¼ cup chia seeds
  • 1 cup light coconut milk
  • ½ cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 dragon fruit (pitaya), peeled and blended
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup or honey
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • Toasted coconut flakes and extra dragon fruit cubes for topping
Instructions
  1. 1 Blend half the dragon fruit with milks, sweetener, and vanilla.
  2. 2 Mix with chia seeds. Refrigerate overnight, stirring once.
  3. 3 Top with remaining dragon fruit cubes and toasted coconut.
