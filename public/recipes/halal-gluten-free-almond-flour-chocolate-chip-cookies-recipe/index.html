Gluten Free Almond Flour Chocolate Chip Cookies — a healthy halal recipe with 140 kcal calories and 4g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups blanched almond flour
  • 1/4 cup coconut oil or butter, softened
  • 1/3 cup monk fruit brown sugar alternative or coconut sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/3 cup sugar-free or dark chocolate chips (halal-certified)
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line baking sheet with parchment.
  2. 2Cream oil/butter and sweetener. Add egg and vanilla.
  3. 3Mix in almond flour, baking soda, and salt. Fold in chips.
  4. 4Scoop and flatten slightly. Bake 12-15 minutes until edges golden. Cool on sheet.
