Meal Prep Indian Spiced Roasted Cauliflower Bowl — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 360 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 large head cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 1 cup cooked chickpeas
  • 2 cups cooked basmati rice or quinoa
  • 1 tsp each turmeric, cumin, garam masala
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Cucumber raita or yogurt (optional), fresh cilantro, lemon
Instructions
  1. 1Toss cauliflower and chickpeas with oil and spices. Roast at 425°F (220°C) for 25-30 minutes.
  2. 2Layer rice in containers, top with roasted cauliflower-chickpea mix.
  3. 3Garnish with cilantro and lemon. Store up to 5 days. Add yogurt sauce when serving.
