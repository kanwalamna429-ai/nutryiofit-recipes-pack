Spinach and Potato Frittata (Mediterranean) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 260 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 8 eggs
  • 4 medium potatoes, diced and parboiled
  • 8 cups fresh spinach
  • 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves
  • ½ cup crumbled feta
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, oregano, salt, pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion and potatoes in oil until golden. Add garlic and spinach; cook until wilted.
  2. 2Whisk eggs with oregano, salt, and pepper. Pour over vegetables. Sprinkle feta on top.
  3. 3Cook on stovetop 4 minutes then bake at 375°F for 20-25 minutes until set.
