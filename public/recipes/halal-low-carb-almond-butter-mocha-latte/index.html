Low Carb Almond Butter Mocha Latte — a healthy halal beverage with 320 calories and 26g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 shot espresso or ½ cup strong brewed coffee
  • 1 tbsp almond butter
  • 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 scoop low-carb chocolate or vanilla protein powder (halal)
  • 1 tbsp MCT oil or coconut oil
  • Ice (for iced version)
Instructions
  1. 1Blend all ingredients until frothy and smooth.
  2. 2For hot: gently warm on stovetop. For iced: blend with ice cubes.
  3. 3Serve immediately.
