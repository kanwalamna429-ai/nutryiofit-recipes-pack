Lebanese Roasted Potatoes with Cilantro and Garlic — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 260 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 6 medium potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • ⅓ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin, paprika, salt, black pepper
  • Lemon wedges
Instructions
  1. 1Toss potato wedges with olive oil, garlic, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. 2Roast at 425°F for 30-35 minutes, flipping halfway, until crispy and golden.
  3. 3Toss hot potatoes with fresh cilantro. Serve with lemon wedges.
