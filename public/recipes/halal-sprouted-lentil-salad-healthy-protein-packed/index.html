Sprouted Lentil Salad (Healthy Protein Packed) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 280 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups sprouted lentils (or store-bought)
  • 1 bell pepper, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, carrot
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • Fresh herbs (parsley, cilantro)
  • Dressing: 3 tbsp olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, salt, pepper, pinch cumin
Instructions
  1. 1If sprouting at home, soak lentils 8 hours then rinse twice daily for 2-3 days until sprouted.
  2. 2Lightly blanch sprouts 1-2 minutes if desired for easier digestion, then cool.
  3. 3Toss sprouts with chopped vegetables, avocado, herbs, and dressing. Serve fresh.
