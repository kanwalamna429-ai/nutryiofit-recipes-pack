Chickpea Shakshuka with Harissa — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 380 calories and 20g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas
  • 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
  • 1-2 tbsp harissa paste (adjust spice)
  • 1 onion, 3-4 garlic cloves
  • Spices: cumin, paprika, salt
  • 4-6 eggs (optional; omit for vegan)
  • Olive oil, fresh herbs
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion/garlic. Add harissa and spices. Stir in tomatoes and chickpeas; simmer 10-15 minutes.
  2. 2Make wells and crack eggs in (if using); cover and cook until set. Garnish with herbs. Serve with bread.
