Air Fryer Eggplant Fries (Low Calorie) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 140 calories and 3g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 medium eggplants, cut into fry shapes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder, paprika, oregano
  • 3 tbsp cornstarch or breadcrumbs
  • Salt and pepper
  • Marinara or yogurt dip for serving
Instructions
  1. 1Toss eggplant fries with oil, spices, salt, and cornstarch.
  2. 2Air fry at 400°F (205°C) for 12-18 minutes, shaking basket often, until golden and tender.
  3. 3Serve hot with dipping sauce.
