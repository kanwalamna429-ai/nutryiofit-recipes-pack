Mediterranean Zucchini and Rice Pilaf Recipe Greek Style — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 260 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup long-grain rice, rinsed
  • 2 medium zucchinis, diced
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp dried dill and mint
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Fresh parsley
  • Salt and black pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Heat olive oil in a pot. Sauté onion until soft. Add garlic and zucchini; cook 4-5 minutes.
  2. 2Add rice and stir 1-2 minutes. Pour in broth, salt, and pepper. Bring to boil.
  3. 3Simmer covered 18-20 minutes until rice is tender.
  4. 4Fluff rice, stir in lemon juice and fresh herbs. Rest 5 minutes before serving.
