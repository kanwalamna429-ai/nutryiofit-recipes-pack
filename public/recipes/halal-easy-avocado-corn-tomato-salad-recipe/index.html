Easy Avocado Corn Tomato Salad Recipe

Easy Avocado Corn Tomato Salad Recipe — a healthy halal recipe with 220 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 ripe avocados, diced
  • 2 cups corn kernels (fresh or thawed)
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ red onion, diced
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro and parsley
  • Dressing: 3 tbsp olive oil, juice of 2 limes, 1 garlic clove, salt & pepper, pinch cumin.
Instructions
  1. 1Gently combine avocado, corn, tomatoes, onion, and herbs.
  2. 2Whisk dressing.
  3. 3Drizzle over salad and toss lightly to preserve avocado texture. Serve fresh.
