Lentil Taco Filling (Spiced Cumin) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 180 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup brown or green lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp oregano
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • Salt, lime juice, fresh cilantro
  • Taco shells or tortillas
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion and garlic. Add spices and tomato paste; cook 1 minute.
  2. 2Stir in lentils and broth. Simmer 18-22 minutes until lentils are tender and liquid mostly absorbed.
  3. 3Mash slightly for texture. Finish with lime juice and cilantro. Use in tacos with toppings.
