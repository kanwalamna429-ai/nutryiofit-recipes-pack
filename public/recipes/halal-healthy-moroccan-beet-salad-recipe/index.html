Healthy Moroccan Beet Salad Recipe — a healthy halal recipe with 160 calories and 4g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4-5 medium beets, roasted and diced
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro and parsley, chopped
  • ¼ cup toasted almonds or walnuts
  • Optional: orange segments
  • Dressing: 3 tbsp olive oil, juice of 1 orange + 1 lemon, ½ tsp cumin, ½ tsp paprika, ¼ tsp cinnamon, salt & pepper.
Instructions
  1. 1Roast beets at 400°F for 40 min. Cool, peel, and dice.
  2. 2Whisk dressing. Toss beets with herbs and nuts.
  3. 3Drizzle spiced dressing and mix well.
