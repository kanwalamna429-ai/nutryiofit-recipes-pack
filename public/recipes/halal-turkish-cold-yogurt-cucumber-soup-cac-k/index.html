Turkish Cold Yogurt Cucumber Soup (Cacık) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 140 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups plain yogurt (full fat or Greek)
  • 2-3 cucumbers, grated and drained
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, salt to taste
  • 1/2 tsp dried mint, cold water as needed
Instructions
  1. 1Grate cucumbers, sprinkle with salt and let drain 10 min. Squeeze out excess water.
  2. 2In a bowl, whisk yogurt with garlic, olive oil, salt and a little cold water to desired consistency.
  3. 3Stir in grated cucumber and dill.
  4. 4Chill for at least 30 min. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle dried mint before serving.
