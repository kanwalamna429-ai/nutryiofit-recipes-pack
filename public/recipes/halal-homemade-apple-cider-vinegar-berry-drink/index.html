Homemade Apple Cider Vinegar Berry Drink — a healthy halal beverage with 100 calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar (with mother)
  • 1 cup cold water or sparkling water
  • 1-2 tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Ice cubes
Instructions
  1. 1Blend berries with a little water until smooth.
  2. 2Mix berry puree with apple cider vinegar, lemon juice, honey, and remaining liquid.
  3. 3Serve over ice. Shake well before drinking.
