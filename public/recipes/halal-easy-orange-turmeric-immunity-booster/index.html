Easy Orange Turmeric Immunity Booster — a healthy halal recipe with 110 calories and 2g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 3 large oranges, peeled
  • 1-inch fresh turmeric root
  • 1-inch fresh ginger
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Pinch of black pepper
  • ½ cup coconut water
Instructions
  1. 1 Juice oranges, turmeric, ginger, and lemon or blend and strain.
  2. 2 Stir in black pepper and coconut water.
  3. 3 Serve fresh as a shot or full drink.
