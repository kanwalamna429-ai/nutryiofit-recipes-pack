Tofu and Eggplant Stir Fry (Ginger Sesame) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 230 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 (14 oz) firm tofu, cubed
  • 1 large eggplant, cubed (salt and drain 10 min)
  • 3 garlic cloves + 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce/tamari, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch (for crisp)
  • Sesame seeds, green onions
Instructions
  1. 1Toss tofu with cornstarch. Stir-fry in oil until crispy; remove.
  2. 2Stir-fry eggplant until soft. Add ginger-garlic.
  3. 3Return tofu, add sauce; toss 2–3 min. Garnish.
  4. 4Serve over rice.
