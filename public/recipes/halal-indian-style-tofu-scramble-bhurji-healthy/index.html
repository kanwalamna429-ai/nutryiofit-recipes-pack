Indian Style Tofu Scramble Bhurji - Healthy — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 200 calories and 20g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 400g firm tofu, crumbled
  • 1 onion, tomato, green chili
  • Turmeric, cumin, garam masala, chili powder
  • Ginger-garlic optional
  • Oil, salt, cilantro, optional veggies (peas/peppers)
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion, aromatics, tomato, spices.
  2. 2Add crumbled tofu; stir-fry 5-8 min until scrambled and spiced. Healthy protein boost.
