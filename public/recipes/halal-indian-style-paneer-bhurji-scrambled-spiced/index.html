Indian Style Paneer Bhurji (Scrambled Spiced) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 260 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 250g paneer, crumbled
  • 1 onion, 2 tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 1 green chili, 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ½ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp garam masala, ½ tsp chili powder
  • 2 tbsp oil or butter, salt, fresh cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Heat oil, sauté onion and green chili until soft. Add ginger-garlic and tomatoes.
  2. 2Cook spices 2–3 min. Add crumbled paneer and salt.
  3. 3Stir-fry 5–7 min until paneer is well coated and heated through.
  4. 4Garnish with cilantro. Serve with toast, paratha, or roti.
