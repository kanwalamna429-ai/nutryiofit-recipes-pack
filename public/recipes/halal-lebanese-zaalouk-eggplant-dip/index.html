Lebanese Zaalouk - Eggplant Dip — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 110 calories and 3g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 large eggplants
  • 3-4 tomatoes, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, 1/2 tsp chili flakes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, salt, fresh cilantro or parsley, lemon juice
Instructions
  1. 1Roast or grill eggplants until soft. Peel and chop pulp.
  2. 2Heat olive oil. Sauté garlic, add tomatoes, spices and cook down.
  3. 3Add eggplant pulp and mash together. Cook 10 min until thick. Finish with lemon juice and herbs. Serve warm or cold.
