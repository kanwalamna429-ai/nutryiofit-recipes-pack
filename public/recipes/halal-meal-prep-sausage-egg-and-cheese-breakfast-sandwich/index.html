Meal Prep Sausage Egg and Cheese Breakfast Sandwich — a healthy halal recipe with 420 calories and 28g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb halal breakfast sausage (chicken or turkey)
  • 6 eggs
  • 6 English muffins or gluten-free buns, toasted
  • 6 slices cheddar or American cheese
  • 1 cup spinach or arugula
  • Salt, pepper, optional hot sauce
Instructions
  1. 1 Form sausage into 6 patties and cook in skillet until done (internal 165°F).
  2. 2 Fry or scramble eggs in rings for uniform shape. Season well.
  3. 3 Assemble: bottom muffin, spinach, sausage patty, egg, cheese, top muffin.
  4. 4 Wrap individually and refrigerate up to 4 days. Reheat in microwave or air fryer.
