Easy Chicken Avgolemono Soup (Lemon) — a healthy halal recipe with 270 calories and 26g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lb halal chicken breast, diced or shredded
  • 1 onion, diced; 2 carrots, sliced
  • 6 cups halal chicken broth
  • ½ cup orzo or rice
  • 3 eggs
  • Zest and juice of 2-3 lemons
  • Fresh dill, salt & pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion and carrots 5 min. Add chicken and broth; simmer 15 min.
  2. 2Add orzo/rice; cook until tender. Remove from heat.
  3. 3Whisk eggs with lemon juice. Temper with hot broth, then stir into soup. Heat gently (do not boil). Garnish with dill.
