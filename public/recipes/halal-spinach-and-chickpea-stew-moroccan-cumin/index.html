Spinach and Chickpea Stew (Moroccan Cumin) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 280 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained
  • 8-10 cups fresh spinach
  • 1 onion, 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 (14 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp ground cumin, 1 tsp paprika, ½ tsp cinnamon, chili flakes
  • Salt, fresh cilantro, lemon wedges
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion and garlic in olive oil. Add spices and cook 1 minute.
  2. 2Stir in tomatoes and chickpeas; simmer 10 minutes.
  3. 3Add spinach in batches until wilted. Simmer 5 more minutes. Serve with lemon and cilantro.
