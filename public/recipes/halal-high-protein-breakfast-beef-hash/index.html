High Protein Breakfast Beef Hash — a healthy halal recipe with 380 calories and 28g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb lean ground beef (halal)
  • 2 medium potatoes, diced small
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper
  • 4 eggs (optional, fried on top)
  • Fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1 Brown beef in large skillet, drain excess fat if needed. Remove and set aside.
  2. 2 In same skillet, cook potatoes, onion, and pepper with seasonings until tender.
  3. 3 Return beef to skillet and mix well. Cook 5 more minutes.
  4. 4 Top with fried eggs if desired and parsley.
