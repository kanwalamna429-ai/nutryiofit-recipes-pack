Low Calorie Strawberry Chia Jam — a healthy halal recipe with 40 kcal calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups fresh or frozen strawberries
  • 2 Tbsp chia seeds
  • 1-2 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1-2 Tbsp monk fruit sweetener (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Simmer strawberries in a saucepan over medium heat, mashing until broken down (~8-10 min).
  2. 2Stir in chia seeds, lemon, and sweetener. Cook 2 more minutes.
  3. 3Cool and refrigerate; thickens as it chills. Store up to 1 week.
