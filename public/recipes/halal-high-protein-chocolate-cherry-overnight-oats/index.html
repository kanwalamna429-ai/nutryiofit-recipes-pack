High Protein Chocolate Cherry Overnight Oats — a healthy halal recipe with 420 calories and 28g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder (unsweetened)
  • 1 cup pitted cherries (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 scoop chocolate protein powder
  • 1 tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • Dark chocolate chips (halal) for topping
Instructions
  1. 1 Mix oats, milk, yogurt, cocoa, chia seeds, protein powder, and sweetener.
  2. 2 Fold in half the cherries. Divide into jars.
  3. 3 Top with remaining cherries and chocolate chips.
  4. 4 Refrigerate overnight. Stir before eating.
