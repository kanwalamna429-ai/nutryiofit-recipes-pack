Refreshing Cantaloupe Mint Cooler — a healthy halal beverage with 110 calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 cups ripe cantaloupe chunks
  • 10-12 fresh mint leaves
  • Juice of 1-2 limes
  • 1 cup cold water or coconut water
  • 1 tbsp honey (optional)
  • Ice and mint sprigs for garnish
Instructions
  1. 1Blend cantaloupe, mint, lime juice, and honey until smooth.
  2. 2Strain for clearer texture or serve pulpy.
  3. 3Add cold water, stir, and serve over ice.
