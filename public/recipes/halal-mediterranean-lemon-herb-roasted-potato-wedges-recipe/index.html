Mediterranean Lemon Herb Roasted Potato Wedges Recipe — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 280 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 large Yukon gold or russet potatoes, cut into wedges
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • Juice and zest of 2 lemons
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Fresh parsley for garnish
  • Salt and black pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 425°F. Line baking sheet with parchment.
  2. 2Toss potato wedges with olive oil, lemon zest and juice, garlic, oregano, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  3. 3Arrange in single layer. Roast 35-40 minutes, flipping halfway, until crispy and golden.
  4. 4Garnish with fresh parsley and extra lemon wedges.
