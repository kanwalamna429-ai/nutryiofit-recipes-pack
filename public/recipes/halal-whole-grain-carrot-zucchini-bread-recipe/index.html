Whole Grain Carrot Zucchini Bread — a healthy halal recipe with 180 kcal calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup grated carrots + 1 cup grated zucchini (squeezed)
  • 1½ cups whole wheat flour
  • ½ cup oats
  • 2 eggs
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup + ¼ cup applesauce
  • ¼ cup oil
  • 1 tsp baking soda, 1 tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp vanilla, pinch salt
  • Optional: ½ cup walnuts, raisins
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat 350°F. Grease loaf pan.
  2. 2Mix veggies with wet ingredients. Combine with dry.
  3. 3Bake 45-55 min. Cool before slicing.
