Low Carb Zucchini Lasagna with Ricotta Recipe (Vegetarian) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 280 calories and 18g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4-5 large zucchini, sliced lengthwise thinly
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese (or cottage cheese)
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella (halal)
  • 1 (24 oz) jar low-sugar marinara sauce
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • Fresh basil
  • Olive oil spray
  • Salt and pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Salt zucchini slices and let drain 15 minutes; pat dry.
  2. 2Mix ricotta with egg, garlic, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
  3. 3Layer in baking dish: sauce, zucchini, ricotta mix, mozzarella. Repeat, ending with cheese.
  4. 4Bake covered at 375°F for 30 minutes, then uncovered 15 minutes until bubbly and golden. Rest 10 minutes before slicing.
