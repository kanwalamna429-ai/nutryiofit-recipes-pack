Baked Blueberry Lemon Poppyseed Oatmeal — a healthy halal recipe with 280 calories and 9g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 2 cups milk or almond milk
  • 2 large eggs
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 cups fresh blueberries
  • 2 tbsp poppy seeds
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • 1 tsp baking powder, vanilla, salt
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease 9x9 baking dish.
  2. 2 Whisk milk, eggs, maple, lemon zest/juice, vanilla.
  3. 3 Mix in oats, baking powder, salt, poppy seeds. Fold in blueberries.
  4. 4 Pour into dish. Bake 35-40 minutes until golden and set.
  5. 5 Serve warm with yogurt drizzle.
