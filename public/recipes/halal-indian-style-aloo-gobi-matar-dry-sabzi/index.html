Indian Style Aloo Gobi Matar (Dry Sabzi) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 180 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 potatoes, ½ head cauliflower (florets), ½ cup green peas
  • 1 onion, 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp coriander powder, ½ tsp garam masala, chili powder
  • 3 tbsp oil, salt, fresh cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Heat oil, add cumin. Sauté onion, then tomato and spices.
  2. 2Add potatoes and cauliflower with 2–3 tbsp water. Cover and cook 12–15 min.
  3. 3Add peas and cook uncovered 5–7 min until tender and dry.
  4. 4Garnish with cilantro. Serve with roti.
