Indian Style Moong Dal Cheela (Protein Breakfast) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 220 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup split moong dal (soaked 30 min)
  • 1 green chili, 1 inch ginger
  • ½ tsp cumin, salt
  • Oil for cooking
  • Optional filling: chopped onion, tomato, cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Blend soaked dal with ginger, chili, cumin, and salt into smooth batter.
  2. 2Heat non-stick pan, pour ladleful batter and spread thin like dosa.
  3. 3Drizzle oil, add optional toppings, cook both sides until golden and crispy.
  4. 4Serve hot with chutney or yogurt.
