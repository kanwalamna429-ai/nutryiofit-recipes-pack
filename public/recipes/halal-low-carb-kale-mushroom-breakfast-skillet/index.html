Low Carb Kale Mushroom Breakfast Skillet — a healthy halal recipe with 260 calories and 16g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 eggs
  • 4 cups kale, chopped
  • 2 cups mushrooms, sliced
  • ½ small onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, red pepper flakes
  • ¼ cup feta cheese (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1 Heat oil in large skillet. Sauté onion and mushrooms 5 minutes.
  2. 2 Add garlic and kale, cook until kale wilts.
  3. 3 Make wells and crack eggs in. Cover and cook until eggs reach desired doneness.
  4. 4 Season and top with feta if using.
