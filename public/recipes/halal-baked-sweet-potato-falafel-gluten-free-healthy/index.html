Baked Sweet Potato Falafel (Gluten Free Healthy) — a healthy halal recipe with 380 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 large sweet potato (about 12 oz), baked or steamed
  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
  • 3 garlic cloves, ½ small onion
  • ½ cup fresh parsley & cilantro
  • 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, ½ tsp paprika
  • 3-4 tbsp chickpea flour or almond flour, salt & pepper
  • Olive oil spray
  • Bowl/Serving: Mixed greens, cucumber-tomato salad, tahini dressing, pickled veggies.
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Mash baked sweet potato. Pulse chickpeas, garlic, onion, herbs in food processor.
  2. 2Mix with sweet potato, spices, and flour to form a soft dough. Shape into patties.
  3. 3Place on lined baking sheet, spray with oil, bake 25-30 min, flipping halfway until golden and firm.
  4. 4Serve in bowls with fresh veggies and tahini.
