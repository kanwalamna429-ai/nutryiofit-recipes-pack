Gluten Free Chicken Broccoli Casserole — a healthy halal recipe with 390 kcal calories and 34g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 lbs halal chicken thighs, cooked and shredded
  • 6 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 cup heavy cream or coconut cream
  • 2 cups shredded cheese (or dairy-free)
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 375°F. Steam broccoli 3-4 minutes.
  2. 2Mix chicken, broccoli, cream, half the cheese, garlic, and seasonings in a bowl.
  3. 3Transfer to greased casserole dish. Top with remaining cheese.
  4. 4Bake 25-30 minutes until bubbly and golden.
