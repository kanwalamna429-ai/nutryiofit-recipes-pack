Oven Baked Raspberry Cheesecake Overnight Oats — a healthy halal recipe with 380 calories and 18g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 2 cups milk or almond milk
  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup maple syrup or honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 1 1/2 cups fresh or frozen raspberries
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened (or more Greek yogurt)
  • 1/4 cup sliced almonds
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease 8x8 baking dish.
  2. 2 Mix oats, milk, yogurt, eggs, sweetener, vanilla, and lemon zest. Fold in half the raspberries and dollop cream cheese.
  3. 3 Top with remaining raspberries and almonds.
  4. 4 Bake 25-30 minutes until set. Serve warm or chill overnight.
