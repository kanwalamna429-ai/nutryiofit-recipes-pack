Hearty Vegetable Minestrone Soup (Italian Style) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 230 calories and 9g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 zucchini, 1 cup green beans, chopped
  • 1 (28 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 1 (15 oz) can white beans or kidney beans
  • ½ cup small pasta (ditalini)
  • 6 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, Italian herbs, garlic, fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion, carrot, celery in oil. Add garlic and herbs.
  2. 2Stir in tomatoes, broth, and beans; simmer 15 min.
  3. 3Add zucchini, green beans, and pasta; cook until pasta is tender.
  4. 4Garnish with parsley and serve with grated Parmesan (optional).
