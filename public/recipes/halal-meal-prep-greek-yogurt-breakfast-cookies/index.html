Meal Prep Greek Yogurt Breakfast Cookies — a healthy halal recipe with 260 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract, cinnamon, baking soda
  • 1 cup mixed berries or chocolate chips (halal)
  • 1/2 cup chopped almonds or walnuts
  • Pinch of salt
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 350°F. Line baking sheets.
  2. 2 Mix yogurt, eggs, sweetener, vanilla, cinnamon, baking soda, and salt.
  3. 3 Stir in oats, nuts, and berries until combined.
  4. 4 Scoop 12 cookies onto sheets. Bake 15-18 minutes until golden. Cool completely.
  5. 5 Store in fridge up to 5 days.
