Indian Style Aloo Posto (Bengali Potato Poppy Seed) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 180 calories and 4g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium potatoes, cubed
  • 3 tbsp poppy seeds (posto), soaked and ground to paste
  • 1–2 green chilies
  • ½ tsp nigella seeds (kalonji), ½ tsp turmeric
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil, salt, fresh cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Heat mustard oil. Add nigella seeds and green chilies.
  2. 2Add potato cubes, turmeric, and salt. Stir-fry 5 min.
  3. 3Add poppy seed paste and ½ cup water. Cover and cook on low 12–15 min until potatoes are tender.
  4. 4Garnish with cilantro. Serve with rice and dal.
