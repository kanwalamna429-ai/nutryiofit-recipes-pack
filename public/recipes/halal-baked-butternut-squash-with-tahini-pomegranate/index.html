Baked Butternut Squash with Tahini Pomegranate — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 240 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 large butternut squash, peeled and cubed
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin, paprika, salt
  • 1/4 cup tahini
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • Water to thin tahini
  • Pomegranate seeds, fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Toss squash cubes with oil, spices, and salt. Spread on baking sheet.
  2. 2Roast at 425°F (220°C) for 35-40 minutes until caramelized.
  3. 3Whisk tahini sauce with lemon, garlic, and water. Drizzle over roasted squash. Top with pomegranate seeds and parsley.
