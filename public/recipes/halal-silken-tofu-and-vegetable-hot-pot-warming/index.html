Silken Tofu and Vegetable Hot Pot (Warming) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 140 calories and 9g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 (12–14 oz) package silken tofu, cubed
  • 4 cups mixed vegetables (napa cabbage, carrots, mushrooms, spinach)
  • 4–5 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce or tamari
  • 1 tbsp ginger, 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Green onions and chili oil (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Simmer broth with ginger, garlic, and soy sauce for 5 min.
  2. 2Add harder vegetables (carrots, mushrooms) and cook 5 min.
  3. 3Gently add silken tofu and leafy greens; simmer 3–5 min more.
  4. 4Finish with sesame oil and garnish. Serve immediately.
