Easy Banana Oat Energy Muffins — a healthy halal recipe with 160 kcal calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1½ cups old-fashioned oats (blended into flour or partial)
  • ½ cup whole wheat flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch salt
  • 2 eggs
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • ¼ cup olive oil or almond butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Optional: ⅓ cup raisins or chocolate chips
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 375°F. Line muffin tin.
  2. 2Mix dry ingredients.
  3. 3Whisk mashed bananas, eggs, maple, oil, and vanilla.
  4. 4Combine and fold in add-ins.
  5. 5Bake 18-22 minutes. Cool on rack.
