Healthy Fig Jam Breakfast Toast — a healthy halal recipe with 380 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 slices whole grain or sourdough bread
  • ½ cup homemade or store-bought fig jam (no added sugar or alcohol)
  • ½ cup ricotta cheese or Greek yogurt
  • 2 fresh figs, sliced (or dried)
  • 2 tbsp chopped walnuts or almonds
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Pinch of cinnamon
  • Fresh thyme or mint for garnish
Instructions
  1. 1 Toast bread slices until golden.
  2. 2 Spread ricotta or yogurt on each slice, then layer with fig jam.
  3. 3 Top with fresh fig slices, chopped nuts, a drizzle of honey, and cinnamon.
  4. 4 Garnish with herbs and serve immediately.
