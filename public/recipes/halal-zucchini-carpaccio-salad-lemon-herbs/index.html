Zucchini Carpaccio Salad (Lemon Herbs) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 160 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 3 medium zucchini, very thinly sliced (mandoline recommended)
  • ¼ cup shaved parmesan or nutritional yeast
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • Zest and juice of 1 large lemon
  • 2 tbsp fresh basil and mint, torn
  • 1 garlic clove, very finely minced
  • Salt, black pepper, pine nuts (toasted)
Instructions
  1. 1Arrange zucchini slices overlapping on a large platter.
  2. 2Whisk olive oil, lemon zest, juice, garlic, salt, and pepper. Drizzle over zucchini.
  3. 3Scatter herbs, shaved parmesan, and pine nuts. Let sit 5-10 minutes before serving for flavors to meld.
