Moroccan Spiced Sweet Potato Salad Recipe — a healthy halal recipe with 310 calories and 9g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 large sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 1 (15-oz) can chickpeas, drained
  • ½ cup dried apricots or raisins, chopped
  • ¼ cup almonds, toasted and sliced
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro and parsley, chopped
  • 4 cups baby spinach
  • Dressing: ¼ cup olive oil, juice of 1 orange + 1 lemon, 1 tsp honey, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp paprika, salt & pepper.
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss sweet potatoes with 1 tbsp oil and spices. Roast 25-30 min.
  2. 2Whisk remaining dressing. Combine roasted sweet potatoes, chickpeas, dried fruit, nuts, and spinach.
  3. 3Drizzle dressing and toss gently.
