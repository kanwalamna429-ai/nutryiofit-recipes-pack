Easy Moroccan Carrot Chickpea Salad — a healthy halal recipe with 260 calories and 9g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4-5 large carrots, shredded or julienned
  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
  • ½ red onion, chopped
  • ⅓ cup raisins or dried apricots
  • ¼ cup fresh cilantro and parsley
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, juice of 1 lemon, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp paprika, salt & honey to taste
Instructions
  1. 1Whisk oil, lemon, spices, and honey for dressing.
  2. 2Combine carrots, chickpeas, onion, raisins, and herbs in a bowl.
  3. 3Pour dressing over and toss well. Let sit 10 min before serving.
