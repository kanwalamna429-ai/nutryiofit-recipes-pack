Meal Prep Cauliflower Rice Burrito Bowls (Vegetarian) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 320 calories and 15g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 large head cauliflower, riced
  • 2 (15 oz) cans black beans
  • 2 cups corn
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 avocado, lime juice, cilantro
  • Spices: cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika
  • Salsa and cheese (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté cauliflower rice with spices 8-10 minutes. Warm beans and corn.
  2. 2Assemble in containers: cauliflower rice base, beans, corn, peppers.
  3. 3Top with avocado, lime, and cilantro just before eating. Store components separately if needed.
