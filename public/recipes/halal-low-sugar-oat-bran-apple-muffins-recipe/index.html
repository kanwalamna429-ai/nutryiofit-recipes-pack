Low Sugar Oat Bran Apple Muffins — a healthy halal recipe with 145 kcal calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup oat bran
  • ¾ cup whole wheat flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda
  • 1½ tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch salt
  • 1 cup grated apples
  • 2 eggs
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • ⅓ cup unsweetened applesauce
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 375°F. Line muffin tin.
  2. 2Mix oat bran, flour, baking powder, soda, cinnamon, and salt.
  3. 3Whisk eggs, maple, applesauce, oil, and vanilla. Stir in grated apples.
  4. 4Combine wet and dry.
  5. 5Divide and bake 18-22 minutes. Cool on rack.
