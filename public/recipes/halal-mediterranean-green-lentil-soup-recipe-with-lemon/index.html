Mediterranean Green Lentil Soup Recipe with Lemon — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 230 calories and 13g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup green lentils, rinsed
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 6 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp each: cumin, coriander
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Juice of 1-2 lemons
  • Fresh parsley and spinach (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Heat oil in a pot. Sauté onion, carrots, and celery for 6-7 minutes. Add garlic and spices.
  2. 2Add lentils, tomatoes, broth, and bay leaves. Bring to boil, then simmer 30-35 minutes until lentils are tender.
  3. 3Stir in spinach if using until wilted. Remove bay leaves.
  4. 4Finish with fresh lemon juice and parsley. Serve hot.
