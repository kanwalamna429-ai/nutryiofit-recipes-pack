Oven Baked Blueberry Cream Cheese Breakfast Danish Recipe Healthy — a healthy halal recipe with 240 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 sheet puff pastry (halal-certified) or homemade dough
  • 1 cup fresh blueberries
  • 4 oz low-fat cream cheese, softened
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup or honey
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 1 egg (for egg wash)
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • Powdered sugar or extra berries for topping (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line baking sheet.
  2. 2 Roll pastry, spread cream cheese mixed with maple, lemon zest, and vanilla, leaving border.
  3. 3 Top with blueberries. Fold edges or cut into squares. Brush with egg wash.
  4. 4 Bake 22-25 minutes until golden. Cool slightly before serving.
