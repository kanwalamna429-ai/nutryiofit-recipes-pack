One Pan Eggplant Tomato Chickpea Bake (Mediterranean) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 300 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 medium eggplants, cubed or sliced
  • 1 (28 oz) can tomatoes (crushed or diced)
  • 1-2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • Garlic, olive oil, oregano, cumin, paprika
  • Fresh parsley, feta (optional, or plant-based)
Instructions
  1. 1Toss eggplant with oil, salt, spices; roast or bake in pan at 400°F (205°C) for 20 minutes.
  2. 2Add tomatoes, chickpeas, onion/garlic. Bake another 20-25 minutes until bubbly and tender.
  3. 3Garnish and serve with bread or couscous.
