Low Calorie Strawberry Kiwi Green Smoothie — a healthy halal recipe with 90 calories and 2g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup strawberries (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 kiwis, peeled
  • 1 cup baby spinach
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • ½ banana (optional for creaminess)
  • Ice cubes
Instructions
  1. 1 Add all ingredients to blender.
  2. 2 Blend until smooth.
  3. 3 Serve chilled.
