Refreshing Pineapple Cucumber Mint Drink — a healthy halal recipe with 200 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups pineapple chunks
  • 1 large cucumber, chopped
  • 10–12 fresh mint leaves
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 cup cold water or coconut water
  • Ice cubes
Instructions
  1. 1 Blend pineapple, cucumber, mint, lime juice, and water until smooth.
  2. 2 Strain for a lighter texture if desired.
  3. 3 Serve over ice with mint garnish.
