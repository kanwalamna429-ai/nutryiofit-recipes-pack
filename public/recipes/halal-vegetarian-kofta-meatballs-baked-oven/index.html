Vegetarian Kofta Meatballs (Baked Oven) — a healthy halal recipe with 280 calories and 15g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas or 1 cup cooked lentils + cauliflower rice
  • 1 onion, 3 garlic, fresh herbs (parsley/cilantro)
  • 2 tsp cumin, coriander, garam masala
  • 3-4 tbsp chickpea flour, salt
  • Olive oil spray
Instructions
  1. 1Process ingredients into mixture. Form balls.
  2. 2Bake at 400°F on lined sheet 20-25 min, turning once.
  3. 3Serve in curry, wraps, or with tahini.
