Meal Prep Pesto Mushroom Egg Cups — a healthy halal recipe with 280 calories and 18g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 8 large eggs
  • 1 cup chopped mushrooms
  • 1 cup chopped spinach or kale
  • ¼ cup basil pesto (check for no parmesan if strict; or use homemade with halal ingredients)
  • ½ cup crumbled feta or shredded mozzarella
  • ¼ cup milk
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder to taste
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease a 12-cup muffin tin.
  2. 2 Sauté mushrooms in olive oil for 5 minutes. Add spinach until wilted.
  3. 3 Whisk eggs, milk, pesto, salt, pepper, and garlic. Stir in veggies and cheese.
  4. 4 Divide into muffin cups. Bake 20-25 minutes until set. Cool and store in fridge for up to 4 days. Reheat in microwave.
