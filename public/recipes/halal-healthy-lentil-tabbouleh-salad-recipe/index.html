Healthy Lentil Tabbouleh Salad Recipe — a healthy halal recipe with 240 calories and 11g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup green lentils
  • 2 large bunches parsley, finely chopped
  • ½ cup fresh mint, chopped
  • 4 tomatoes, finely diced
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • 1 cucumber, finely diced
  • Dressing: ⅓ cup olive oil, juice of 3 lemons, salt & pepper.
Instructions
  1. 1Cook lentils in water until tender but firm (~20 min). Drain and cool.
  2. 2Combine cooled lentils with finely chopped herbs and vegetables.
  3. 3Add olive oil, lemon juice, salt & pepper. Toss thoroughly. Adjust lemon to taste.
