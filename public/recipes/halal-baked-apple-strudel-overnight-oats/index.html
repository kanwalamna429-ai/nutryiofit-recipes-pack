Baked Apple Strudel Overnight Oats — a healthy halal recipe with 350 calories and 10g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1/2 cup applesauce
  • 2 apples, diced
  • 1/3 cup maple syrup
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1/4 cup chopped walnuts or pecans
  • 2 tbsp butter or coconut oil, melted
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease baking dish.
  2. 2 Mix oats, milk, applesauce, sweetener, cinnamon, and vanilla. Fold in diced apples.
  3. 3 Top with nuts and drizzle melted butter.
  4. 4 Bake 25-30 minutes. Serve warm or refrigerate.
