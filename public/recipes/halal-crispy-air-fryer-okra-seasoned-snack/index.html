Crispy Air Fryer Okra Seasoned Snack — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 140 calories and 4g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb fresh okra, sliced lengthwise or whole small pods
  • 2 tbsp olive oil or avocado oil
  • ½ cup chickpea flour or cornmeal
  • 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp smoked paprika, ½ tsp cayenne
  • Salt & black pepper
  • Lemon wedges
Instructions
  1. 1Toss okra with oil and seasonings, then coat lightly in chickpea flour.
  2. 2Preheat air fryer to 400°F. Arrange in single layer.
  3. 3Air fry 12–15 min, shaking halfway, until crispy.
  4. 4Serve immediately with lemon.
