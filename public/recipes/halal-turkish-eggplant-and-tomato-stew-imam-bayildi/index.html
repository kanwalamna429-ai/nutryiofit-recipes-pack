Turkish Eggplant and Tomato Stew (Imam Bayildi) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 240 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium eggplants
  • 2 large onions, sliced
  • 4-5 tomatoes, chopped
  • 6 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1/2 cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp sugar optional, salt, black pepper
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
Instructions
  1. 1Peel eggplants in stripes and halve lengthwise. Fry or brush with oil and bake until soft.
  2. 2In a pan, sauté onions and garlic in olive oil until golden. Add tomatoes, parsley, salt and pepper. Cook down.
  3. 3Place eggplants in a baking dish. Top with tomato-onion mixture.
  4. 4Bake covered at 375°F (190°C) for 30-40 min. Serve at room temperature.
