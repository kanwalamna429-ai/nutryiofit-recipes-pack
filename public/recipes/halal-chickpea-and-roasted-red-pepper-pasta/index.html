Chickpea and Roasted Red Pepper Pasta — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 480 calories and 20g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 12 oz pasta (regular or chickpea pasta)
  • 2 (12 oz) jars roasted red peppers, drained
  • 1-2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas
  • 3-4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil or tahini for creaminess
  • Fresh basil, oregano, red pepper flakes, salt
  • Pasta water to loosen
Instructions
  1. 1Cook pasta. Blend most roasted peppers, garlic, chickpeas (some whole reserved), oil/tahini into sauce.
  2. 2Sauté reserved chickpeas and peppers. Toss with cooked pasta and sauce, adding pasta water for silky texture. Garnish with basil.
