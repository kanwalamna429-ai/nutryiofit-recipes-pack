Baked Blueberry Lemon Protein Muffins — a healthy halal recipe with 140-170 kcal calories and 10g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 ½ cups oat flour or almond flour blend (gluten-free)
  • 2 scoops vanilla protein powder
  • 1 cup blueberries (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt or applesauce
  • ¼ cup maple syrup
  • Zest + juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch salt
