Creamy Peanut Butter Banana Protein Shake — a healthy halal recipe with 420 calories and 32g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 large frozen banana
  • 2 tbsp natural peanut butter (no added sugar)
  • 1 scoop halal vanilla protein powder
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • Ice cubes
Instructions
  1. 1 Add banana, peanut butter, protein powder, almond milk, and cinnamon to a blender.
  2. 2 Blend on high until thick and creamy.
  3. 3 Add ice for extra thickness if desired and blend again. Serve immediately.
