Creamy Avocado Banana Chocolate Smoothie — a healthy halal recipe with 320 calories and 4g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • ½ ripe avocado
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 tbsp honey or date syrup (optional)
  • Ice as needed
Instructions
  1. 1 Scoop avocado flesh into blender.
  2. 2 Add banana, cocoa, almond milk, and sweetener.
  3. 3 Blend until thick and creamy. Adjust thickness with ice.
