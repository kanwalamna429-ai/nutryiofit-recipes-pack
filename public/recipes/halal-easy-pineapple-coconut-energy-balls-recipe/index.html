Easy Pineapple Coconut Energy Balls — a healthy halal recipe with 180 kcal calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened shredded coconut
  • 1/2 cup dried pineapple (no added sugar), chopped
  • 1/3 cup almond butter
  • 2 Tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2-3 Tbsp unsweetened coconut milk (as needed)
Instructions
  1. 1Mix oats, coconut, pineapple, and flaxseed.
  2. 2Add almond butter and vanilla. Stir in coconut milk until dough forms.
  3. 3Roll into 12 balls. Chill 30 minutes. Store in fridge.
