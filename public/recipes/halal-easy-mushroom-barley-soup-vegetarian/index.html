Easy Mushroom Barley Soup (Vegetarian) — a healthy halal recipe with 220 calories and 7g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 8-12 oz mushrooms (cremini or button), sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¾ cup pearl barley, rinsed
  • 6 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp thyme, salt & pepper
  • Fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion, carrots, mushrooms until golden. Add garlic.
  2. 2Add barley and broth; simmer 40-45 min until barley is tender.
  3. 3Season and garnish. (Barley thickens it naturally.)
