Easy Roasted Garlic Breakfast Potatoes — a healthy halal recipe with 220 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium potatoes, cubed
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh parsley for garnish
  • Optional: fried eggs on top
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. 2 Toss potato cubes with oil, garlic, paprika, rosemary, salt, and pepper on a sheet pan.
  3. 3 Roast 30-35 minutes, flipping halfway, until crispy and golden.
  4. 4 Garnish with parsley and serve with eggs if desired.
