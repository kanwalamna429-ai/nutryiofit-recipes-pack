Creamy Spinach Artichoke Soup — a healthy halal recipe with 160 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 (12 oz) jar artichoke hearts, drained and chopped
  • 6 cups fresh spinach
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 cup coconut milk or cashew cream
  • Salt, pepper, pinch nutmeg
  • Fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion 5 min. Add garlic and artichokes 3 min.
  2. 2Add spinach and broth; simmer 15 min until spinach wilts.
  3. 3Blend until creamy. Stir in coconut milk, season, and heat gently. Garnish with parsley.
