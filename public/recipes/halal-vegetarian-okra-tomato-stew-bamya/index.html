Vegetarian Okra Tomato Stew (Bamya) — a healthy halal recipe with 170 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb fresh or frozen okra, trimmed
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 (28 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, ½ tsp paprika, salt & pepper
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, fresh cilantro, lemon wedges
Instructions
  1. 1Heat oil in a pot. Sauté onion until soft. Add garlic and spices; cook 1 min.
  2. 2Stir in tomato paste, then add diced tomatoes and 1 cup water. Bring to simmer.
  3. 3Add okra and cook 25-30 min until tender, stirring gently occasionally.
  4. 4Finish with fresh cilantro and lemon juice. Serve with rice or flatbread.
