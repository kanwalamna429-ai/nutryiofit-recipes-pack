Homemade Cantaloupe Cucumber Cooler — a healthy halal beverage with 100 calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 cups ripe cantaloupe chunks
  • ½ large English cucumber, chopped
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 8-10 fresh mint leaves
  • 1 cup cold water or coconut water
  • Ice cubes
Instructions
  1. 1Blend cantaloupe, cucumber, lime juice, and mint until smooth.
  2. 2Strain if desired and mix with cold water.
  3. 3Serve over ice with mint garnish.
