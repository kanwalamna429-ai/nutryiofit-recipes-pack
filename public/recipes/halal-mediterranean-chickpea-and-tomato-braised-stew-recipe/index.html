Mediterranean Chickpea and Tomato Braised Stew Recipe — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 280 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp each: cumin, smoked paprika, oregano
  • Fresh parsley and lemon
  • Salt and black pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion in olive oil until golden. Add garlic and spices.
  2. 2Stir in tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, and chickpeas. Simmer 20-25 minutes.
  3. 3Finish with lemon juice and fresh parsley.
