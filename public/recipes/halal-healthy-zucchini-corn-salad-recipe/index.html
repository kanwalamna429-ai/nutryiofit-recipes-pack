Healthy Zucchini Corn Salad Recipe — a healthy halal recipe with 150 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 3 medium zucchinis, spiralized or diced
  • 2 cups corn kernels (fresh or thawed)
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ¼ cup fresh basil and mint
  • Optional: ½ cup halal feta
  • Dressing: 3 tbsp olive oil, juice of 2 limes, 1 garlic clove, salt & pepper.
Instructions
  1. 1Whisk dressing.
  2. 2Combine zucchini, corn, tomatoes, and herbs.
  3. 3Drizzle dressing and toss. Add feta if desired. Serve fresh.
