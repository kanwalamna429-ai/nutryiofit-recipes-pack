Healthy Baked Cherry Oatmeal Crumble Recipe

Healthy Baked Cherry Oatmeal Crumble — a healthy halal recipe with 195 kcal calories and 4g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 cups fresh or frozen pitted cherries
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 Tbsp arrowroot
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/4 cup almond flour
  • 1/4 cup chopped almonds
  • 2 Tbsp coconut oil, melted
  • 1-2 Tbsp monk fruit sweetener
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Toss cherries with lemon, cinnamon, and arrowroot in baking dish.
  2. 2Mix topping and sprinkle over filling.
  3. 3Bake 30 minutes until bubbly and golden. Let cool slightly before serving.
