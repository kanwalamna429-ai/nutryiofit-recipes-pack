Vegetarian Stuffed Acorn Squash (Quinoa) — a healthy halal recipe with 380 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 medium acorn squash, halved and seeded
  • 1 cup quinoa, cooked
  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, 1 apple or cranberries, chopped
  • 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves, spinach or kale
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp thyme, ½ tsp cinnamon, salt & pepper
  • Toasted pecans or walnuts
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven 400°F. Brush squash with oil, season, and roast cut-side down 35-45 min until tender.
  2. 2Sauté onion and garlic. Add chickpeas, apple, greens, cooked quinoa, and spices.
  3. 3Fill roasted squash halves with mixture. Bake 10 more min. Top with nuts.
