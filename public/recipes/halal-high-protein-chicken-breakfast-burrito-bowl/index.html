High Protein Chicken Breakfast Burrito Bowl — a healthy halal recipe with 450 calories and 35g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb ground halal chicken (seasoned with taco spices)
  • 4 eggs, scrambled
  • 2 cups cooked quinoa or cauliflower rice
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • Salsa, black beans (rinsed), corn, cilantro, lime
  • Cheese, Greek yogurt as "sour cream"
Instructions
  1. 1 Cook chicken with spices until done. Scramble eggs separately.
  2. 2 Assemble bowls: base of quinoa/rice, top with chicken, eggs, beans, veggies, avocado, and toppings.
  3. 3 Meal prep in containers.
