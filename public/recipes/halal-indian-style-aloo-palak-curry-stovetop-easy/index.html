Indian Style Aloo Palak Curry (Stovetop Easy) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 220 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium potatoes, cubed
  • 1 lb fresh spinach, pureed or finely chopped
  • 1 onion, 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp coriander, ½ tsp garam masala, chili powder
  • 3 tbsp oil, salt
Instructions
  1. 1Boil or lightly fry potato cubes; set aside.
  2. 2Heat oil, add cumin. Sauté onion, ginger-garlic, tomatoes, and spices.
  3. 3Add spinach puree and cook 5–7 min. Add potatoes and simmer 8–10 min.
  4. 4Serve with roti or rice.
