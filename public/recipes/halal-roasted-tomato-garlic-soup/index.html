Roasted Tomato Garlic Soup — a healthy halal recipe with 150 calories and 4g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 3 lbs Roma tomatoes, halved
  • 1 large onion, quartered; 8 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, 1 tsp dried basil
  • 3-4 cups vegetable broth
  • Fresh basil leaves
Instructions
  1. 1Roast tomatoes, onion, and garlic with oil, salt, pepper, and basil at 425°F for 40 min.
  2. 2Blend roasted vegetables with broth until smooth.
  3. 3Simmer 10 min. Stir in fresh basil and adjust seasoning.
