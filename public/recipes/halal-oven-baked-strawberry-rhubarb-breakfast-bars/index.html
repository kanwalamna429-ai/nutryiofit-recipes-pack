Oven Baked Strawberry Rhubarb Breakfast Bars — a healthy halal recipe with 210 calories and 4g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups chopped strawberries
  • 1 cup chopped rhubarb
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 1½ cups rolled oats
  • ½ cup almond flour
  • ⅓ cup chopped walnuts
  • ¼ cup coconut oil, melted
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line an 8x8 pan with parchment.
  2. 2 Mix strawberries, rhubarb, 2 tbsp maple, and lemon zest. Spread in pan.
  3. 3 Combine oats, almond flour, walnuts, oil, 3 tbsp maple, cinnamon, and salt. Sprinkle evenly over fruit.
  4. 4 Bake 30-35 minutes until golden. Cool completely before cutting.
