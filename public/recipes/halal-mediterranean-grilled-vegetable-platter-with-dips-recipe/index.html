Mediterranean Grilled Vegetable Platter with Dips Recipe — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 180 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 zucchinis, 1 eggplant, 2 bell peppers, 1 red onion (sliced thick)
  • 1 bunch asparagus (optional)
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 2 tsp dried oregano and thyme
  • Salt and pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Toss sliced vegetables with olive oil, herbs, salt, and pepper.
  2. 2Grill on medium-high heat 3-5 minutes per side until charred and tender.
  3. 3Arrange on a large platter.
  4. 4Serve with variety of Mediterranean dips, extra olive oil, and lemon wedges.
