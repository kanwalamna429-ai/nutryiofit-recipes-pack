Baked Vegetable Kofta Curry — a healthy halal recipe with 360 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • Kofta: Mixed vegetables (1 carrot, 1 potato, ½ cauliflower) grated, ½ cup peas, ½ cup chickpea flour, 2 tsp garam masala, salt, oil spray.
  • Curry: 1 onion, 3 garlic, 1 tbsp ginger, 1 (15 oz) can tomato sauce, 1 cup coconut milk, 2 tsp curry powder, spices.
Instructions
  1. 1Mix grated vegetables (squeeze moisture), peas, flour, and spices. Form balls and bake at 400°F for 20-25 min.
  2. 2Sauté onion, garlic, ginger, and spices. Add tomato sauce and simmer 10 min. Stir in coconut milk.
  3. 3Add baked koftas and simmer gently 8-10 min. Garnish with cilantro. Serve with rice.
