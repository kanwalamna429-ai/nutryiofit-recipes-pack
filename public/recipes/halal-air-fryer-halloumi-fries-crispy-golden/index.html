Air Fryer Halloumi Fries (Crispy Golden) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 260 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 8-10 oz halloumi cheese, cut into thick fries/sticks
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano or za'atar
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika (optional)
  • Fresh lemon wedges and mint for serving
Instructions
  1. 1Pat halloumi dry and toss sticks gently with olive oil and seasonings.
  2. 2Arrange in single layer in air fryer basket (work in batches if needed).
  3. 3Air fry at 390°F (200°C) for 8-12 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crispy outside, soft inside.
  4. 4Serve immediately with lemon wedges, fresh mint, and optional yogurt dip.
