Gluten Free Roasted Chickpea Salad Recipe (Healthy) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 320 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained and dried
  • 4 cups mixed greens
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ red onion, sliced
  • ¼ cup olives
  • ¼ cup olive oil (divided)
  • 1 tsp each: cumin, paprika, garlic powder
  • Lemon-tahini dressing
  • Salt and pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Toss chickpeas with 2 tbsp olive oil and spices. Roast at 425°F for 22-25 minutes until crispy.
  2. 2Combine greens, cucumber, tomatoes, onion, and olives in a bowl.
  3. 3Top with roasted chickpeas and drizzle with lemon-tahini dressing. Toss gently.
