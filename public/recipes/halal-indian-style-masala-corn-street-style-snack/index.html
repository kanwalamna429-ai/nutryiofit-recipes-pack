Indian Style Masala Corn (Street Style Snack)

Indian Style Masala Corn (Street Style Snack) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 160 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 cups sweet corn kernels (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 tbsp butter or oil
  • 1 tsp chaat masala, ½ tsp chili powder, ½ tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice, salt
  • 2 tbsp chopped onion, tomato, cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Boil or steam corn until tender. Drain well.
  2. 2Toss hot corn with butter, chaat masala, chili powder, cumin, and salt.
  3. 3Mix in chopped onion, tomato, cilantro, and lemon juice.
  4. 4Serve immediately as snack.
