Meal Prep Baked Falafel with Tahini and Veggies — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 360 calories and 16g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas
  • 1 small onion, 4 garlic cloves, ½ cup parsley
  • 2 tsp cumin and coriander
  • 3 tbsp chickpea flour
  • Salt, pepper, olive oil spray
  • Tahini sauce, cucumber, tomatoes, greens for bowls
Instructions
  1. 1Pulse chickpeas, onion, garlic, herbs, and spices. Mix in flour. Form patties.
  2. 2Spray with oil and bake at 400°F (205°C) for 22-25 minutes, flipping once.
  3. 3Cool and portion with chopped veggies and tahini sauce containers. Refrigerate up to 5 days. Reheat falafel if desired.
