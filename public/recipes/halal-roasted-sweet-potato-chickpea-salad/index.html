Roasted Sweet Potato Chickpea Salad — a healthy halal recipe with 320 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 large sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 2 (15-oz) cans chickpeas, drained
  • 4 cups baby spinach or arugula
  • ¼ cup pumpkin seeds, toasted
  • ½ red onion, sliced
  • Dressing: ¼ cup olive oil, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp cumin, 1 garlic clove, salt & pepper.
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven 425°F. Toss sweet potatoes with 1 tbsp oil and salt. Roast 25-30 min.
  2. 2Whisk dressing. Combine roasted sweet potatoes, chickpeas, greens, onion, and seeds.
  3. 3Drizzle dressing and toss.
