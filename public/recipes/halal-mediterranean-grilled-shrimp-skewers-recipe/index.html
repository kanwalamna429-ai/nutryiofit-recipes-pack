Mediterranean Grilled Shrimp Skewers — a healthy halal recipe with 260 kcal calories and 32g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs halal large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • Juice and zest of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp dried oregano and paprika
  • ½ tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Wooden skewers (soaked in water)
  • Fresh parsley and lemon wedges
Instructions
  1. 1Mix olive oil, garlic, lemon juice/zest, oregano, paprika, salt, and pepper. Toss shrimp in marinade for 15-20 minutes.
  2. 2Thread shrimp onto skewers.
  3. 3Preheat grill to medium-high. Grill 3-4 minutes per side until pink and opaque.
  4. 4Garnish with parsley and serve with lemon wedges.
