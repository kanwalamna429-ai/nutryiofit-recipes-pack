Lentil and Roasted Potato Stew (Moroccan) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 340 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup green lentils
  • 4-5 medium potatoes, cubed
  • 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 (14 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 tsp ras el hanout or cumin + paprika + cinnamon
  • Olive oil, preserved lemon (optional), fresh cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Roast potato cubes with oil and salt at 425°F for 25 minutes.
  2. 2Sauté onion and garlic. Add spices, tomatoes, lentils, and broth. Simmer 20 minutes.
  3. 3Stir in roasted potatoes and cook 5 more minutes. Garnish with cilantro and preserved lemon.
