One Pan Okra Tomato Sauce (Lebanese Style) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 180 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb fresh okra, trimmed
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 (28 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Fresh cilantro or parsley for garnish
Instructions
  1. 1Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add onion and sauté until soft.
  2. 2Add garlic and spices; cook 1 minute. Stir in tomatoes and bring to simmer.
  3. 3Add okra, cover, and cook 15-20 minutes until okra is tender but not mushy. Finish with lemon juice and herbs. Serve with rice or warm bread.
