Healthy Pineapple Mango Breakfast Salsa with Eggs — a healthy halal recipe with 320 calories and 15g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup pineapple, diced
  • 1 cup mango, diced
  • ½ red bell pepper, diced
  • ¼ red onion, finely chopped
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced (optional)
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Fresh cilantro, salt, pepper
  • 1 tsp olive oil
Instructions
  1. 1 Mix pineapple, mango, bell pepper, onion, jalapeño, lime juice, cilantro, salt. Set aside.
  2. 2 Heat oil in skillet. Cook eggs to preference (fried or scrambled).
  3. 3 Serve eggs topped generously with fruit salsa.
  4. 4 Garnish with extra cilantro.
