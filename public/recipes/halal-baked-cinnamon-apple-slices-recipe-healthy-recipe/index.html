Baked Cinnamon Apple Slices Recipe (Healthy) — a healthy halal recipe with 120 kcal calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium apples, cored and sliced into thin wedges
  • 1-2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 Tbsp coconut oil or avocado oil, melted
  • Optional: Pinch nutmeg, 1 Tbsp chopped walnuts
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line baking sheet.
  2. 2Toss apple slices with cinnamon and oil.
  3. 3Arrange in single layer. Bake 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, until tender and lightly crisp.
