Zucchini and Feta Pancakes (Quick Lunch) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 220 calories and 11g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 medium zucchini, grated and squeezed dry
  • ½ cup crumbled feta cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • ⅓ cup flour (or chickpea flour)
  • 2 scallions, chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tsp dried dill or oregano, salt, pepper
  • Olive oil for cooking
Instructions
  1. 1Mix grated zucchini, feta, eggs, flour, scallions, garlic, and seasonings into a thick batter.
  2. 2Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Spoon batter to form small pancakes.
  3. 3Cook 3-4 minutes per side until golden and crisp. Serve warm with yogurt, tzatziki, or salad.
