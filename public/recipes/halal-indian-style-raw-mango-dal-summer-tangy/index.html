Indian Style Raw Mango Dal (Summer Tangy) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 210 calories and 11g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup toor dal or moong dal
  • 1 medium raw mango, peeled and cubed
  • 1 onion, 2 green chilies
  • ½ tsp turmeric, ½ tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds, 1 tsp cumin, curry leaves, 2 tbsp ghee
Instructions
  1. 1Cook dal with turmeric until soft. Add raw mango and simmer 8–10 min.
  2. 2Heat ghee, add mustard, cumin, curry leaves, onion, and chilies. Sauté until golden.
  3. 3Add chili powder and pour tadka into dal. Mix well.
  4. 4Serve with rice.
