Baked Vegetarian Kibbeh (Bulgur Healthy) — a healthy halal recipe with 320 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup fine bulgur, rinsed
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas or 2 cups cooked lentils, drained
  • ½ cup walnuts or pine nuts, chopped
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp allspice, ½ tsp cinnamon, salt & pepper
  • Fresh parsley and mint, 2 tbsp pomegranate molasses (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Soak bulgur in hot water for 10-15 min, then drain well. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. 2Pulse chickpeas/lentils with half the onion and spices in food processor. Mix with bulgur, remaining onion, herbs, nuts, and 2 tbsp oil.
  3. 3Press mixture into an oiled 9x13 baking dish or form into patties. Drizzle with remaining oil and score diamond pattern. Bake 30-40 min until golden and firm. Serve with yogurt or salad.
