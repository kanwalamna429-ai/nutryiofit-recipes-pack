Easy Roasted Garlic Potato Soup — a healthy halal recipe with 220 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 lbs potatoes, cubed
  • 1 whole garlic bulb
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 5 cups vegetable broth
  • ½ cup coconut milk or oat milk
  • Salt, pepper, fresh chives
Instructions
  1. 1Toss potatoes, onion, and garlic bulb (top sliced off, drizzled with oil) with salt and pepper. Roast at 425°F for 35-40 min.
  2. 2Squeeze roasted garlic from cloves. Blend potatoes, onion, garlic with broth.
  3. 3Simmer 10 min. Stir in coconut milk and garnish with chives.
