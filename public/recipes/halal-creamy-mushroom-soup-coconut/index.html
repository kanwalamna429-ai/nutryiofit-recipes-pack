Creamy Mushroom Soup (Coconut) — a healthy halal recipe with 180 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1.5 lbs mixed mushrooms (cremini, button), sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 (13.5 oz) can coconut milk
  • Salt, pepper, fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Heat oil; sauté onion 5 min. Add mushrooms; cook until golden (8-10 min).
  2. 2Add garlic and thyme 2 min. Pour in broth; simmer 15 min.
  3. 3Blend partially or fully. Stir in coconut milk and season.
