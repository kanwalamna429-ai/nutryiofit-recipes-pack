Air Fryer Eggplant Parmesan (Less Oil) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 280 calories and 16g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 large eggplant, sliced ½-inch thick
  • 1 cup marinara sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, olive oil spray
Instructions
  1. 1Dip eggplant slices in egg then seasoned breadcrumbs. Spray both sides with oil.
  2. 2Air fry at 380°F (193°C) for 12-15 minutes, flipping halfway.
  3. 3Top with marinara and cheeses. Air fry 3-5 more minutes until cheese melts. Garnish with basil.
