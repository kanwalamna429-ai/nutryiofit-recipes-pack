Easy Banana Cinnamon Warm Drink — a healthy halal recipe with 160 calories and 3g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 ripe banana, mashed
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • 1 tsp honey or maple syrup
  • ¼ tsp vanilla extract
Instructions
  1. 1 Warm almond milk in a saucepan.
  2. 2 Stir in mashed banana, cinnamon, nutmeg, honey, and vanilla.
  3. 3 Simmer gently for 3–4 minutes, whisking until smooth.
  4. 4 Pour into a mug and enjoy warm.
