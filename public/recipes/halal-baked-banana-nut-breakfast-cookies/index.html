Baked Banana Nut Breakfast Cookies — a healthy halal recipe with 310 calories and 9g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • ½ cup chopped walnuts or pecans
  • ⅓ cup almond butter
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • Pinch of salt
  • Optional: ¼ cup raisins
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line baking sheet.
  2. 2 Mix all ingredients until combined.
  3. 3 Scoop and flatten slightly on sheet.
  4. 4 Bake 15 minutes until golden. Cool before serving.
