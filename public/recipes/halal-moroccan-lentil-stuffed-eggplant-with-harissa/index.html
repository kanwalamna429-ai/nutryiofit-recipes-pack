Moroccan Lentil Stuffed Eggplant with Harissa — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 260 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium eggplants, halved lengthwise
  • 1 cup green lentils, cooked
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1-2 tsp harissa paste
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, salt
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, fresh parsley, 1/4 cup chopped walnuts optional
Instructions
  1. 1Scoop out eggplant flesh, leaving a shell. Chop flesh.
  2. 2Sauté onion and garlic in olive oil. Add chopped eggplant, lentils, harissa and spices. Cook 8-10 min.
  3. 3Stuff eggplant shells with mixture. Place in baking dish, drizzle with oil.
  4. 4Cover and bake at 400°F (200°C) for 30-35 min until eggplants are tender. Garnish with parsley.
