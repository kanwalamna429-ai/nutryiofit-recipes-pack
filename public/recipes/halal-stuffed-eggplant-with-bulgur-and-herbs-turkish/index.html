Stuffed Eggplant with Bulgur and Herbs (Turkish) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 320 calories and 9g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium eggplants, halved lengthwise
  • 1 cup bulgur wheat
  • 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves, 1 bell pepper, tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, Turkish spices (cumin, allspice, paprika)
  • Fresh parsley, mint, dill, lemon zest
  • 1½ cups vegetable broth or water
Instructions
  1. 1Scoop out eggplant flesh, chop it, and sauté with onion, garlic, pepper, and tomatoes. Add bulgur and herbs; cook 2 minutes.
  2. 2Stuff mixture into eggplant halves. Place in baking dish, drizzle with oil, and pour broth around.
  3. 3Cover and bake at 375°F for 40-45 minutes until eggplants are soft. Uncover last 10 minutes.
