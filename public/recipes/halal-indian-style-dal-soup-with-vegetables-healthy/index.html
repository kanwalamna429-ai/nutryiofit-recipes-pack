Indian Style Dal Soup with Vegetables (Healthy) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 200 calories and 11g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • ¾ cup mixed dal (moong + masoor), rinsed
  • 1 carrot, 1 zucchini, handful spinach (chopped)
  • 1 onion, 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, 1 tsp turmeric, ½ tsp cumin powder
  • 2 tbsp ghee or oil, salt, fresh cilantro, lemon
Instructions
  1. 1Cook dal with turmeric in 4 cups water until soft.
  2. 2Heat ghee, sauté onion, ginger-garlic, and tomato. Add vegetables and cook 5 min.
  3. 3Add cooked dal and simmer 10–12 min. Add spinach in last 3 min.
  4. 4Garnish with cilantro and lemon. Serve as light soup or with rice.
