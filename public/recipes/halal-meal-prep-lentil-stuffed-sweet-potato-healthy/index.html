Meal Prep Lentil Stuffed Sweet Potato (Healthy) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 340 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium sweet potatoes
  • 1 ½ cups cooked green lentils
  • 1 cup spinach, wilted
  • ½ cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 small red onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp tahini or olive oil
  • Cumin, salt, pepper, lemon juice
Instructions
  1. 1Bake sweet potatoes at 425°F for 40-45 minutes until soft.
  2. 2Mix lentils with spinach, tomatoes, onion, tahini, spices, and lemon.
  3. 3Split potatoes, stuff with lentil mixture. Cool and store in containers. Reheat in microwave.
