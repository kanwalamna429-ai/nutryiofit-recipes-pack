Easy Pineapple Coconut Green Smoothie — a healthy halal beverage with 280 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup frozen pineapple chunks
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 2 cups fresh baby spinach
  • 1 cup light coconut milk (canned or carton, unsweetened)
  • ½ cup unsweetened almond milk or water
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Place all ingredients in a blender.
  2. 2Blend until smooth and vibrant green. Add more liquid if needed.
  3. 3Serve fresh for the best tropical flavor.
