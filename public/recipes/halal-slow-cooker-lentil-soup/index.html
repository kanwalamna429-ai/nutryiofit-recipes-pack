Slow Cooker Lentil Soup — a healthy halal recipe with 220 calories and 13g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 onion, diced; 3 carrots, sliced; 2 celery, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1.5 cups green or brown lentils, rinsed
  • 1 (15 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 6-7 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp Italian seasoning, bay leaf
  • 2 cups spinach, chopped
  • Salt, pepper, lemon juice
Instructions
  1. 1Add all ingredients except spinach and lemon to slow cooker.
  2. 2Cook on low 6-8 hours (or high 3-4 hours).
  3. 3Stir in spinach until wilted. Finish with lemon juice and seasoning.
