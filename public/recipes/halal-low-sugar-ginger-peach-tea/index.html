Low Sugar Ginger Peach Tea — a healthy halal recipe with 50 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 peach herbal tea bags or black tea
  • 2 ripe peaches, sliced
  • 1-inch fresh ginger, sliced
  • 4 cups water
  • Monk fruit sweetener to taste
  • Ice cubes
Instructions
  1. 1 Boil water and steep tea with ginger and peach slices for 7 minutes.
  2. 2 Remove tea bags, mash peaches lightly, and strain.
  3. 3 Sweeten, chill, and serve over ice.
