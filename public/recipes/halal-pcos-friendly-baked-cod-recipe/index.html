PCOS Friendly Baked Cod — a healthy halal recipe with 240 kcal calories and 28g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 halal cod fillets (6 oz each)
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • Juice and zest of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp dried oregano and turmeric
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. 2Place cod and tomatoes in a baking dish. Drizzle with oil, garlic, lemon, oregano, turmeric, salt, and pepper.
  3. 3Bake 15-20 minutes until fish flakes easily. Garnish with parsley.
