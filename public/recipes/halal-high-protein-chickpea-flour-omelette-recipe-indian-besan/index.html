High Protein Chickpea Flour Omelette Recipe (Indian Besan) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 320 calories and 18g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup chickpea flour (besan)
  • 1 cup water (or more for consistency)
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1-2 green chilies, chopped (optional)
  • ½ tsp each: turmeric, cumin, garam masala
  • ¼ tsp baking soda (for fluffiness)
  • Handful fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil or ghee
  • Salt to taste
  • Optional: spinach or bell pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Whisk chickpea flour, water, spices, salt, and baking soda into a smooth batter. Let rest 5 minutes. Stir in veggies and cilantro.
  2. 2Heat oil in a non-stick pan. Pour half the batter, spread into a thin omelette. Cook 3-4 minutes per side until golden and set.
  3. 3Repeat for second omelette. Fold and serve with chutney, Greek yogurt, or salad.
