Lebanese Mujaddara - Red Lentil Version with Crispy Onions — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 380 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 cup rice (basmati or short grain), rinsed
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp coriander powder, 1/2 tsp cinnamon optional
  • 3-4 tbsp olive oil, salt to taste, fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Cook lentils in 3 cups water until soft. Add rice, spices and salt. Cook until rice is tender (add water as needed).
  2. 2Meanwhile, heat olive oil and fry sliced onions until deep golden and crispy.
  3. 3Fluff mujaddara and top generously with crispy onions and parsley. Serve warm.
