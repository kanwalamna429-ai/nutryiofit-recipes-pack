Low Sugar Coconut Lime Chia Pudding — a healthy halal recipe with 240 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1/4 cup chia seeds
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) light coconut milk
  • Zest and juice of 1 lime
  • 1-2 tbsp monk fruit sweetener or maple
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • Toppings: Toasted coconut, lime slices, fresh mango
Instructions
  1. 1 Whisk chia seeds, coconut milk, lime zest/juice, sweetener, and vanilla.
  2. 2 Refrigerate, stirring after 30 minutes. Chill until thickened.
  3. 3 Top with toasted coconut and mango before serving.
