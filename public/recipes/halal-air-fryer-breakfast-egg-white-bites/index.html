Air Fryer Breakfast Egg White Bites — a healthy halal recipe with 110 calories and 16g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 12 egg whites
  • 1 cup chopped spinach
  • ½ cup diced bell peppers
  • ½ cup shredded mozzarella
  • ¼ cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder
  • Cooking spray
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat air fryer to 350°F (175°C). Spray silicone muffin cups.
  2. 2 Whisk egg whites with cottage cheese and seasonings.
  3. 3 Stir in spinach, peppers, and mozzarella.
  4. 4 Fill cups and air fry 12-15 minutes until set.
