Baked Cauliflower Steaks with Harissa — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 180 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 large cauliflowers, cut into 1-inch steaks
  • 3-4 tbsp harissa paste
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin, smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper
  • Lemon wedges and fresh herbs for serving
Instructions
  1. 1Mix harissa, oil, and spices. Brush both sides of cauliflower steaks generously.
  2. 2Place on baking sheet. Bake at 425°F (220°C) for 25-30 minutes, flipping once, until caramelized and tender.
  3. 3Serve with yogurt (optional), lemon, and herbs alongside couscous or salad.
