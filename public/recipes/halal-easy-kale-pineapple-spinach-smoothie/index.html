Easy Kale Pineapple Spinach Smoothie — a healthy halal beverage with 210 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups fresh kale leaves (stems removed)
  • 2 cups fresh spinach
  • 1 cup frozen pineapple chunks
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk or coconut water
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Add kale, spinach, pineapple, banana, almond milk, lemon juice, and chia seeds to a blender.
  2. 2Blend on high until completely smooth and bright green.
  3. 3Serve immediately for best nutrients and taste.
