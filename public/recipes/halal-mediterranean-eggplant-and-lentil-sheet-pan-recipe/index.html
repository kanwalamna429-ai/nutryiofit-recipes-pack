Mediterranean Eggplant and Lentil Sheet Pan Recipe — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 260 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 medium eggplants, cubed
  • 1 cup green lentils, cooked
  • 1 red onion, wedged
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes
  • 4 garlic cloves, smashed
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp each: oregano, thyme, cumin
  • 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • Fresh basil or parsley
  • Salt and pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss eggplant, onion, tomatoes, and garlic with olive oil, herbs, salt, and pepper on a large sheet pan.
  2. 2Roast 35-40 minutes, stirring halfway, until eggplant is tender.
  3. 3Gently mix in cooked lentils and balsamic vinegar.
  4. 4Garnish with fresh herbs before serving.
