Turkish Hummus with Spiced Chickpea Topping — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 290 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • Hummus: 2 cans chickpeas (reserve 1/3 cup), 1/2 cup tahini, 3 garlic cloves, juice of 1 large lemon, 1/4 cup olive oil, salt, ice water
Instructions
  1. 1Blend chickpeas, tahini, garlic, lemon juice and salt. Gradually add olive oil and ice water until ultra creamy.
  2. 2For topping: Sauté reserved chickpeas with spices and olive oil until warmed and fragrant.
  3. 3Spread hummus in a bowl, make a well in center and add spiced chickpeas. Drizzle extra olive oil.
  4. 4Garnish with parsley. Serve with warm pita.
