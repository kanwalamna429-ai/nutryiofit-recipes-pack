Low Sugar Apple Cinnamon Bread — a healthy halal recipe with 160-180 kcal calories and 4g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 large apples, shredded or diced (about 1½-2 cups)
  • 1½ cups white whole wheat flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda
  • 2 tsp cinnamon (plus more for swirl)
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup unsweetened applesauce
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup or monk fruit sweetener
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Optional: ¼ cup chopped walnuts
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat 350°F. Grease loaf pan.
  2. 2Mix apples with some cinnamon. Whisk dry ingredients.
  3. 3Beat eggs, applesauce, sweetener, oil, vanilla. Combine with dry, fold in apples.
  4. 4Pour in pan, optional cinnamon swirl on top. Bake 45-55 min. Cool fully.
