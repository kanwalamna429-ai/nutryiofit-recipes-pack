Healthy Strawberry Spinach Salad Balsamic — a healthy halal recipe with 220 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 6 cups baby spinach
  • 2 cups strawberries, sliced
  • ⅓ cup walnuts or pecans, toasted
  • ½ small red onion, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup halal feta, crumbled (optional)
  • Dressing: ¼ cup olive oil, 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, salt & pepper.
Instructions
  1. 1Toast nuts until fragrant. Cool.
  2. 2Whisk dressing.
  3. 3Layer spinach, strawberries, onion, nuts, and feta. Drizzle dressing right before serving.
