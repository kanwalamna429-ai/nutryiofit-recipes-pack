Quinoa Breakfast Porridge — a healthy halal breakfast with 280 calories and 11g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • Berries to top
Instructions
  1. 1Simmer quinoa with milk for 5 minutes.
  2. 2Stir in honey, vanilla, cinnamon.
  3. 3Top with berries.
