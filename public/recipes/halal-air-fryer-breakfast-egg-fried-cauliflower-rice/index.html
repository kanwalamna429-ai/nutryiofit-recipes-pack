Air Fryer Breakfast Egg Fried Cauliflower Rice — a healthy halal recipe with 280 calories and 18g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 3 cups cauliflower rice
  • 4 eggs, beaten
  • ½ cup mixed veggies (peas, carrots, corn – optional)
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • 1 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce or tamari
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt & pepper
  • Optional: halal chicken sausage bits
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat air fryer to 375°F (190°C).
  2. 2 Mix all ingredients in a bowl.
  3. 3 Air fry in a suitable pan/basket for 12-15 minutes, stirring halfway, until eggs cooked and rice lightly browned.
