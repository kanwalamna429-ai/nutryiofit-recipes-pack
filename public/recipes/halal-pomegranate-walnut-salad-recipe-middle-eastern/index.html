Pomegranate Walnut Salad Recipe (Middle Eastern) — a healthy halal recipe with 280 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 6 cups mixed greens or baby spinach
  • 1 cup pomegranate seeds (from 1-2 pomegranates)
  • ¾ cup walnuts, toasted and chopped
  • 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup fresh mint and parsley, chopped
  • Optional: ½ cup halal feta, crumbled
  • Dressing: ¼ cup olive oil, 2 tbsp pomegranate molasses, juice of 1 lemon, 1 tsp honey, salt & pepper.
Instructions
  1. 1Toast walnuts in a dry pan until fragrant (3-5 min). Cool.
  2. 2Whisk dressing ingredients.
  3. 3In a large bowl, combine greens, pomegranate seeds, onion, herbs, and walnuts. Drizzle dressing and toss gently. Add feta if using. Serve immediately.
