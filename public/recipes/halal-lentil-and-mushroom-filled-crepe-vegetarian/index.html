Instructions
  1. 1Make crepe batter; rest 30 minutes if homemade. Cook thin crepes in a non-stick pan.
  2. 2Sauté onion, garlic, and mushrooms until golden. Add cooked lentils and thyme; season well.
  3. 3Fill each crepe with lentil-mushroom mixture, fold, and warm in oven if desired. Serve with salad.
