Low Calorie Stuffed Turkey Breast — a healthy halal recipe with 260 kcal calories and 38g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 3 lb halal boneless turkey breast
  • 4 cups fresh spinach, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup diced mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried thyme and rosemary
  • Salt, black pepper, and paprika
  • Kitchen twine
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. 2Sauté spinach, mushrooms, and garlic in 1 tbsp oil. Season and cool.
  3. 3Butterfly turkey, spread filling, roll and tie with twine. Rub outside with remaining oil and seasonings.
  4. 4Roast 45-55 minutes until internal temperature reaches 165°F. Rest 10 minutes before slicing.
