Low Carb Broccoli Cheddar Egg Bites — a healthy halal recipe with 180 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 8 large eggs
  • 2 cups broccoli florets, finely chopped (steamed)
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar or halal cheese
  • ¼ cup heavy cream or Greek yogurt
  • ½ tsp garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper
  • Olive oil spray
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Spray silicone muffin tray.
  2. 2 Whisk eggs, cream/yogurt, and seasonings.
  3. 3 Mix in broccoli and cheese.
  4. 4 Pour into cups. Bake 18-22 minutes until set.
  5. 5 Cool slightly before removing. Store in fridge.
