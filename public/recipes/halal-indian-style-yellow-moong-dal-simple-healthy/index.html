Indian Style Yellow Moong Dal (Simple Healthy) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 210 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup yellow moong dal (split), rinsed
  • 1 onion, 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp turmeric, ½ tsp red chili powder, ½ tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp ghee or oil, salt, fresh cilantro, lemon wedges
Instructions
  1. 1Heat ghee in a pot. Add cumin seeds, then onion and ginger-garlic paste. Sauté until golden.
  2. 2Add tomato and spices; cook 3–4 min.
  3. 3Add moong dal and 3 cups water. Bring to boil, then simmer 15–20 min until soft and creamy.
  4. 4Garnish with cilantro and lemon. Serve with rice or roti.
