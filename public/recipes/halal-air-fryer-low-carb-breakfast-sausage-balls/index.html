Air Fryer Low Carb Breakfast Sausage Balls — a healthy halal recipe with 260 calories and 24g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb ground halal turkey or chicken sausage
  • 1 cup shredded halal cheddar cheese
  • ½ cup almond flour
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp garlic powder, onion powder, dried sage, black pepper
  • Olive oil spray
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat air fryer to 380°F.
  2. 2 Mix all ingredients thoroughly in a bowl.
  3. 3 Roll into 16 small balls.
  4. 4 Spray lightly with oil and air fry 12-15 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and cooked through.
  5. 5 Serve hot with eggs or as snack.
