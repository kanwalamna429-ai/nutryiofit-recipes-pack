Meal Prep Chickpea and Kale Massaged Salad — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 340 calories and 16g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained
  • 1 large bunch kale, stemmed and chopped
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ¼ cup tahini-lemon dressing (tahini, lemon, garlic, water)
  • 1 tsp cumin, salt, pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Massage kale with a little dressing and salt until tender (2-3 minutes).
  2. 2Add chickpeas, tomatoes, avocado, and remaining dressing. Toss gently.
  3. 3Divide into containers. Keeps fresh in fridge for 4-5 days. Add avocado day-of if preferred.
