Low Calorie Green Apple Kale Smoothie — a healthy halal beverage with 200 calories and 7g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 green apple, cored and chopped
  • 2 cups fresh kale leaves (stems removed)
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Add all ingredients to blender.
  2. 2Blend until smooth (add a splash of water if too thick).
  3. 3Serve fresh for best taste.
