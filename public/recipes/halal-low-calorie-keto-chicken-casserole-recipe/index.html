Low Calorie Keto Chicken Casserole — a healthy halal recipe with 380 kcal calories and 32g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 lbs halal chicken thighs, diced and cooked
  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 cup heavy cream or coconut cream
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar or mozzarella (or dairy-free alternative)
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper
  • Fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. 2Sauté broccoli and garlic in olive oil for 4 minutes. Mix with cooked chicken, cream, half the cheese, and seasonings.
  3. 3Transfer to a greased casserole dish. Top with remaining cheese.
  4. 4Bake 25-30 minutes until bubbly and golden. Garnish with parsley.
