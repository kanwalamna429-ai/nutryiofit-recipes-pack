Keto Beef Zucchini Boats — a healthy halal recipe with 420 kcal calories and 35g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium zucchinis, halved lengthwise
  • 1.5 lbs halal ground beef
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup sugar-free marinara sauce
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh basil
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 400°F. Scoop out zucchini centers.
  2. 2Brown ground beef with garlic, seasoning, salt, and pepper. Stir in marinara.
  3. 3Fill zucchini boats with beef mixture. Drizzle with oil and bake 25-30 minutes. Garnish with basil.
