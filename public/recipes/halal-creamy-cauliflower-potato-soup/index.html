Creamy Cauliflower Potato Soup — a healthy halal recipe with 180 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 large head cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 2 medium potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 5 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 cup coconut milk or oat milk
  • Salt, pepper, pinch nutmeg or chives
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion 5 min. Add garlic, cauliflower, and potatoes 3 min.
  2. 2Add broth and simmer 25 min until very tender.
  3. 3Blend until creamy. Stir in coconut milk, season, and heat gently. Garnish as desired.
