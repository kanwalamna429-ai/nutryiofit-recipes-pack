Eggplant Caprese Stack (Grilled Vegetarian) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 260 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 large eggplants, sliced into ½-inch rounds
  • 8 oz fresh mozzarella, sliced
  • 2-3 ripe tomatoes, sliced
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Balsamic glaze, olive oil, salt, pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Brush eggplant slices with olive oil and season. Grill or pan-sear 3-4 minutes per side until tender with grill marks.
  2. 2Stack: grilled eggplant, tomato slice, mozzarella, basil leaves. Repeat 2-3 times per stack.
  3. 3Drizzle with balsamic glaze and olive oil. Serve immediately.
