Potato and Green Bean Stew (Mediterranean Style) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 220 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 lbs potatoes, cubed
  • 1 lb fresh green beans, trimmed and halved
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 (28 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, ½ tsp cinnamon
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Fresh cilantro or parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Heat olive oil in a large pot. Sauté onion 5 min, add garlic.
  2. 2Add potatoes and spices; cook 5 min. Stir in tomatoes and 2 cups water. Simmer 15 min.
  3. 3Add green beans; simmer another 15–20 min until tender. Adjust seasoning.
  4. 4Garnish with herbs. Serve with rice or bread.
