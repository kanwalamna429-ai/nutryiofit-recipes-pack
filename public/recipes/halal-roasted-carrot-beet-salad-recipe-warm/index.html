Roasted Carrot Beet Salad Recipe Warm — a healthy halal recipe with 260 calories and 7g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium beets, peeled and cubed
  • 4 medium carrots, peeled and sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin, salt & pepper
  • 4 cups arugula or mixed greens
  • ⅓ cup goat cheese or halal feta, crumbled
  • ¼ cup pistachios, chopped
  • Dressing: 3 tbsp olive oil, 2 tbsp balsamic or lemon juice, 1 tsp honey, salt & pepper.
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven 425°F. Toss beets and carrots with oil, cumin, salt & pepper. Roast 35-40 min.
  2. 2Whisk dressing. Place greens on plates, top with warm roasted vegetables, cheese, and pistachios. Drizzle dressing.
