Meal Prep Spinach Mushroom Egg Muffins (Breakfast) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 240 calories and 18g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 8 large eggs
  • 2 cups fresh spinach, chopped
  • 8 oz mushrooms, diced and sautéed
  • ½ cup crumbled feta or shredded cheese
  • ¼ cup milk
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder, olive oil
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté mushrooms. Whisk eggs, milk, and seasonings. Stir in spinach, mushrooms, and cheese.
  2. 2Divide into greased muffin tin.
  3. 3Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 20-25 minutes. Cool and refrigerate up to 5 days. Reheat as needed.
