Instructions
  1. 1Mix grated lauki with besan and spices. Shape into balls and bake at 400°F for 20–25 min.
  2. 2For curry: Sauté onion, ginger-garlic, and blended tomatoes with spices until thick.
  3. 3Add cream and simmer. Gently add baked koftas and heat 5 min.
  4. 4Garnish with cilantro. Serve with roti.
