Moroccan Spiced Pumpkin and Chickpea Soup — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 240 calories and 9g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 lbs pumpkin or butternut squash, cubed
  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, ½ tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp turmeric, ¼ tsp cayenne
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, fresh cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion and garlic in oil. Add spices and cook 1 min.
  2. 2Add pumpkin, chickpeas, and broth. Simmer 25–30 min until pumpkin is soft.
  3. 3Blend half the soup for creaminess, or leave chunky.
  4. 4Garnish with cilantro.
