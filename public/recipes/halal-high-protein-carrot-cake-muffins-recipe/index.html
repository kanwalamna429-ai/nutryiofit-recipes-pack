High Protein Carrot Cake Muffins — a healthy halal recipe with 150 kcal calories and 10g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1½ cups grated carrots
  • 1 cup oat flour
  • ½ cup vanilla protein powder (halal)
  • 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda
  • 1½ tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp nutmeg
  • Pinch salt
  • 3 eggs
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Optional: ½ cup raisins or walnuts
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 375°F. Line muffin tin.
  2. 2Whisk dry ingredients and spices.
  3. 3Mix eggs, maple, yogurt, oil, vanilla, and carrots.
  4. 4Combine with dry. Fold in add-ins.
  5. 5Divide and bake 20-25 minutes. Cool on rack.
