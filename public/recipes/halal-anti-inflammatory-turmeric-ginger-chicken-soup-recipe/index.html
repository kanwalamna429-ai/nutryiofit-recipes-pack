Anti Inflammatory Turmeric Ginger Chicken Soup — a healthy halal recipe with 280 kcal calories and 28g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs halal chicken thighs or breasts
  • 6 cups chicken bone broth (halal)
  • 1 (13.5 oz) can coconut milk (optional for creaminess)
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 carrots, sliced
  • 3 celery stalks, sliced
  • 2 tbsp fresh turmeric, grated (or 1 tsp powder)
  • 2 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • Olive oil, salt, fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion, garlic, ginger, and turmeric in oil in a large pot.
  2. 2Add chicken, broth, carrots, celery, salt, and pepper. Bring to boil, then simmer 25-30 min.
  3. 3Shred chicken, stir in coconut milk if using, and heat through. Garnish with parsley.
