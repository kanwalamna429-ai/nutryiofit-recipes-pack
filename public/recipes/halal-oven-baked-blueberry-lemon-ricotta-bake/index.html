Oven Baked Blueberry Lemon Ricotta Bake — a healthy halal recipe with 260 calories and 16g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese (halal)
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/3 cup maple syrup or honey
  • Zest and juice of 1 large lemon
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 cups fresh or frozen blueberries
  • 1/2 cup rolled oats or almond flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder, pinch salt
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease 8x8 or similar baking dish.
  2. 2 Whisk ricotta, eggs, sweetener, lemon zest/juice, vanilla, baking powder, and salt.
  3. 3 Fold in blueberries. Pour into dish and sprinkle oats on top.
  4. 4 Bake 35-40 minutes until set and lightly golden. Serve warm or chilled.
