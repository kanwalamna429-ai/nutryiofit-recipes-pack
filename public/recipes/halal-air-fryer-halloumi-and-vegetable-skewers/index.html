Air Fryer Halloumi and Vegetable Skewers — a healthy halal recipe with 420 calories and 22g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 14-16 oz halloumi cheese (halal), cubed
  • 1 zucchini, 1 bell pepper, 1 red onion, cherry tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, 2 tsp oregano, 1 tsp garlic powder, lemon zest, salt & pepper
  • Wooden or metal skewers
Instructions
  1. 1Cut vegetables into chunks. Toss halloumi and veggies with oil, herbs, and seasoning.
  2. 2Thread alternately onto skewers.
  3. 3Air fry at 390°F for 10-14 min, turning halfway until halloumi is golden and vegetables tender. Serve with tahini or yogurt sauce.
