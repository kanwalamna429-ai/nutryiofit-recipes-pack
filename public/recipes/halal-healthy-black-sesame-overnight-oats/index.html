Healthy Black Sesame Overnight Oats — a healthy halal recipe with 340 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1 1/2 cups milk or almond milk
  • 2 tbsp black sesame seeds (toasted and ground)
  • 1 tbsp tahini
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup or honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Toppings: Banana slices, extra black sesame, coconut flakes
Instructions
  1. 1 Mix oats, milk, ground black sesame, tahini, sweetener, and vanilla in jars.
  2. 2 Stir well, cover, and refrigerate overnight.
  3. 3 In the morning, stir and add toppings. Thin with extra milk if needed.
