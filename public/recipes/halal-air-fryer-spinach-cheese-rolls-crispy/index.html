Air Fryer Spinach Cheese Rolls (Crispy) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 240 calories and 10g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 8-10 oz fresh spinach, wilted and chopped
  • 1 cup crumbled feta or mozzarella
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tsp dried dill or oregano
  • 8-10 egg roll or spring roll wrappers
  • Olive oil spray
  • Salt and pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Mix spinach, cheese, garlic, herbs, salt, and pepper.
  2. 2Place filling on wrappers, roll tightly, and seal edges with water. Spray lightly with oil.
  3. 3Air fry at 380°F (193°C) for 10-12 minutes until golden and crispy, flipping halfway.
  4. 4Serve with marinara or yogurt dip.
