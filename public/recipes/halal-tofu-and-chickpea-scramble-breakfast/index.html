Tofu and Chickpea Scramble (Breakfast) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 260 calories and 18g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 (14 oz) block firm tofu, crumbled
  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained and lightly mashed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp black salt (kala namak)
  • 2 tbsp nutritional yeast (optional)
  • Oil for cooking, spinach or greens
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion and pepper in oil. Add spices.
  2. 2Add crumbled tofu and chickpeas; cook 8–10 min, stirring.
  3. 3Stir in greens until wilted. Season to taste.
  4. 4Serve with toast or in wraps.
