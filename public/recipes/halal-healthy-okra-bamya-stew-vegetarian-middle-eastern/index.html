Healthy Okra Bamya Stew (Vegetarian Middle Eastern) — a healthy halal recipe with 180 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb fresh or frozen okra, trimmed
  • 1 large onion, diced; 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 (28 oz) can diced tomatoes or fresh
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, ½ tsp paprika
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, salt & pepper
  • Fresh cilantro or parsley, lemon wedges
Instructions
  1. 1Heat oil, sauté onion 5 min. Add garlic and spices for 1 min.
  2. 2Stir in tomato paste, tomatoes, and 1 cup water. Bring to simmer.
  3. 3Add okra and cook 25-30 min until tender (avoid over-stirring to prevent sliminess).
  4. 4Finish with herbs and lemon. Serve with rice or flatbread.
