Whole30 Salmon Sweet Potato Bowl — a healthy halal recipe with 420 kcal calories and 34g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 halal salmon fillets (6 oz each)
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, diced
  • 4 cups baby spinach
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika and garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh herbs
Instructions
  1. 1Toss sweet potatoes with oil, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Roast at 425°F for 20-25 minutes.
  2. 2Season and bake or pan-sear salmon 12-15 minutes.
  3. 3Assemble bowls with spinach, roasted sweet potatoes, and salmon.
