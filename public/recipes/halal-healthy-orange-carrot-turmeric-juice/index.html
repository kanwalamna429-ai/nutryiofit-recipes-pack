Healthy Orange Carrot Turmeric Juice — a healthy halal recipe with 110 calories and 2g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium carrots, peeled
  • 2 large oranges, peeled
  • 1-inch fresh turmeric root (or ½ tsp powder)
  • ½ lemon, peeled
  • 1-inch ginger (optional)
  • ½ cup water or coconut water
Instructions
  1. 1 Juice all ingredients using a juicer.
  2. 2 Alternatively, blend and strain through a fine mesh or nut milk bag.
  3. 3 Stir and serve fresh over ice.
