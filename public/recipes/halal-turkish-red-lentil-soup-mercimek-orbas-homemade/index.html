Turkish Red Lentil Soup (Mercimek Çorbası) - Homemade — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 230 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, 1/2 tsp mint (dried)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil or butter, salt, black pepper
  • Lemon wedges, fresh mint or parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Heat oil in a pot. Sauté onion and carrot until soft. Add tomato paste and spices; cook 1 min.
  2. 2Add lentils and 5 cups water. Bring to boil then simmer 20-25 min until lentils break down.
  3. 3Blend until smooth. Adjust consistency with water.
  4. 4Serve hot with lemon wedges and fresh herbs.
