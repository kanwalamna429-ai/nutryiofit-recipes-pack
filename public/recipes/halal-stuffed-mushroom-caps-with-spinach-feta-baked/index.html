Stuffed Mushroom Caps with Spinach Feta (Baked) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 240 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 12-16 large mushroom caps
  • 4 cups fresh spinach, chopped
  • ½ cup crumbled feta
  • 1 small onion, 3 garlic cloves
  • ½ cup breadcrumbs
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, oregano, salt, pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Remove mushroom stems and chop them. Sauté with onion, garlic, and spinach until wilted. Cool slightly.
  2. 2Mix with feta, breadcrumbs, and seasonings. Stuff into mushroom caps.
  3. 3Drizzle with olive oil and bake at 400°F for 20-25 minutes until golden.
