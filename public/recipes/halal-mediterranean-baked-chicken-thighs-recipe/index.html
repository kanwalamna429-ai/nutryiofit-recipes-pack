Mediterranean Baked Chicken Thighs — a healthy halal recipe with 420 kcal calories and 32g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 lbs halal bone-in chicken thighs
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • ½ cup olives
  • Juice and zest of 1 lemon
  • 2 tsp dried oregano and thyme
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. 2Place chicken and tomatoes in a baking dish. Rub with oil, garlic, lemon, herbs, salt, and pepper. Add olives.
  3. 3Bake 35-45 minutes until skin is crispy and internal temp is 165°F. Garnish with parsley.
