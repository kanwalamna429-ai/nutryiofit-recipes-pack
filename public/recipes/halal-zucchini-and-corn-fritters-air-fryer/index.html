Zucchini and Corn Fritters (Air Fryer) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 220 calories and 9g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 medium zucchini, grated and squeezed dry
  • 1 cup corn kernels (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup flour or chickpea flour
  • 2 scallions, 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin or Italian herbs, salt, pepper
  • Olive oil spray
Instructions
  1. 1Mix grated zucchini, corn, eggs, flour, scallions, garlic, and seasonings into a batter.
  2. 2Preheat air fryer to 390°F. Form small patties and spray with oil.
  3. 3Air fry 12-15 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crisp. Serve with yogurt dip.
