Easy Moroccan Harira Soup (Chickpeas & Lentils) — a healthy halal recipe with 280 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 celery stalks + 1 carrot, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1.5 tsp black pepper, 1.5 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp cinnamon, ¼ tsp ginger
  • 1 (15 oz) can crushed tomatoes
  • ¾ cup lentils (green or brown), rinsed
  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
  • 6-8 cups vegetable broth
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh cilantro + parsley
  • Lemon wedges and harissa (optional, check halal) for serving
  • Salt to taste
Instructions
  1. 1Heat oil; sauté onion, celery, carrot 5-7 min. Add garlic and spices; cook 2 min.
  2. 2Add tomatoes; simmer 5 min. Stir in lentils, chickpeas, and broth.
  3. 3Bring to boil, then simmer 40-50 min until lentils are tender. Stir in herbs. Adjust seasoning.
  4. 4Serve with lemon wedges for brightness.
