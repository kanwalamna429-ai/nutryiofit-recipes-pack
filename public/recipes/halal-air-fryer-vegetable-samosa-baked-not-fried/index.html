Air Fryer Vegetable Samosa (Baked Not Fried) — a healthy halal recipe with 280 calories and 8g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 12-16 square spring roll wrappers or whole wheat wrappers (halal)
  • 2 boiled potatoes, mashed
  • 1 cup peas, 1 carrot finely diced
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp garam masala, ½ tsp turmeric, chili powder, salt
  • 1 tbsp oil + more for brushing
  • Optional: fresh cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté cumin seeds, add vegetables and spices; cook until tender. Mix with mashed potato. Cool.
  2. 2Fill wrappers with 2 tbsp mixture, fold into triangles, seal edges with water. Brush lightly with oil.
  3. 3Air fry at 380°F for 12-15 min, flipping halfway until crispy and golden. Serve with mint chutney or yogurt.
