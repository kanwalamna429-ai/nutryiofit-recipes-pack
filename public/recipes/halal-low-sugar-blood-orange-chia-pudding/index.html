Low Sugar Blood Orange Chia Pudding — a healthy halal recipe with 220 calories and 7g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • ¼ cup chia seeds
  • 1½ cups unsweetened almond milk
  • 1-2 blood oranges (zest + segments)
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup or monk fruit sweetener
  • ½ tsp vanilla
  • Toppings: Pistachios, coconut flakes
Instructions
  1. 1 Whisk chia seeds, milk, sweetener, vanilla, and orange zest.
  2. 2 Refrigerate, stirring after 30 min to prevent clumps. Chill fully.
  3. 3 Top with blood orange segments and nuts before serving.
