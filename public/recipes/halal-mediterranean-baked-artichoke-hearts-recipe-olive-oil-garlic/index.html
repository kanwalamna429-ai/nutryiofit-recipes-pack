Mediterranean Baked Artichoke Hearts Recipe Olive Oil Garlic — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 160 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 (14 oz) cans artichoke hearts, drained and halved
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano and thyme
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan or nutritional yeast (optional)
  • Salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes
  • Fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. 2Toss artichoke hearts with olive oil, garlic, oregano, thyme, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. 3Spread in a baking dish. Sprinkle with cheese or nutritional yeast if using.
  4. 4Bake 20-25 minutes until golden and crispy at edges. Garnish with parsley.
