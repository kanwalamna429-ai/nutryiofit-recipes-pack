Turkish Fried Zucchini Fritters (Mücver) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 240 calories and 9g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 3 medium zucchini, grated
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 small onion, finely grated
  • 3-4 green onions, chopped
  • 1/4 cup fresh dill and parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp dried mint, 1/2 tsp black pepper, salt to taste
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese (halal) optional
  • Olive oil for shallow frying
Instructions
  1. 1Grate zucchini, sprinkle with salt and let drain 10 min. Squeeze out excess moisture thoroughly.
  2. 2In a bowl, mix zucchini with eggs, flour, onion, green onions, herbs, spices and feta (if using) into a thick batter.
  3. 3Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Drop spoonfuls of batter and flatten slightly.
  4. 4Fry 3-4 min per side until golden and crispy. Drain on paper towels. Serve warm with yogurt.
