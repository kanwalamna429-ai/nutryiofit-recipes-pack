Easy Fava Bean Dip (Ful Homemade) — a healthy halal recipe with 260 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 (15 oz) cans fava beans, drained
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ cup olive oil, juice of 2 lemons
  • 1 tsp cumin, salt & pepper, chili flakes
  • Fresh parsley, tomato, onion for topping, warm pita
Instructions
  1. 1Warm fava beans in a pot with a splash of water. Mash roughly with a fork or potato masher.
  2. 2Stir in garlic, cumin, most of the olive oil, and lemon juice. Season well.
  3. 3Top with diced tomato, onion, parsley, chili flakes, and remaining olive oil.
  4. 4Serve warm with pita bread for scooping.
