Vegetarian Moussaka (Eggplant Healthy Light) — a healthy halal recipe with 320 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 large eggplants, sliced ½-inch thick
  • 1 large onion, 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 lb mushrooms or lentils (1.5 cups cooked), chopped
  • 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes, 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp cinnamon, 2 tsp oregano, salt & pepper
  • 2 cups béchamel (2 cups milk + 3 tbsp flour + 2 tbsp oil + nutmeg) or yogurt-tahini sauce for lighter version
  • Olive oil, optional parmesan or nutritional yeast
Instructions
  1. 1Salt eggplant slices, let drain 20 min, rinse and roast at 425°F for 20 min.
  2. 2Sauté onion, add mushrooms/lentils, garlic, spices, tomatoes. Simmer 15 min.
  3. 3Layer eggplant and sauce in baking dish. Top with béchamel. Bake 30-40 min until bubbly and golden.
