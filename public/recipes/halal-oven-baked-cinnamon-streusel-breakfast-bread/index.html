Oven Baked Cinnamon Streusel Breakfast Bread — a healthy halal recipe with 210 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups whole wheat pastry flour or gluten-free blend
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt (plain, halal)
  • 2 eggs
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup or honey
  • ⅓ cup unsweetened applesauce
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract, baking soda, cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt
  • ⅓ cup rolled oats
  • ¼ cup chopped walnuts or pecans
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil or butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp coconut sugar or brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9x5 loaf pan.
  2. 2 Mix wet ingredients (yogurt, eggs, sweetener, applesauce, vanilla). Add dry ingredients until just combined.
  3. 3 Pour batter into pan. Mix streusel and sprinkle evenly on top.
  4. 4 Bake 45-55 minutes until toothpick comes out clean. Cool before slicing.
  5. 5 Great for meal prep; store airtight.
