Low Carb Asparagus Feta Breakfast Tart — a healthy halal recipe with 320 calories and 15g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups almond flour
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/3 cup cold butter, cubed
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 lb asparagus, trimmed
  • 1 cup crumbled feta cheese (halal)
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream or Greek yogurt
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano, salt, black pepper
  • Olive oil
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Mix almond flour, Parmesan, and butter until crumbly. Press into a greased 9-inch tart pan. Bake crust 10 minutes.
  2. 2 Whisk eggs, cream/yogurt, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  3. 3 Arrange asparagus on crust, pour egg mixture over, sprinkle feta.
  4. 4 Bake 25-30 minutes until set and golden. Cool slightly before slicing.
