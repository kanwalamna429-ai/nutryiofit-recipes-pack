Gluten Free Baked Cauliflower Fritters Recipe — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 210 calories and 9g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 medium head cauliflower, riced and squeezed dry
  • 2 large eggs
  • ½ cup almond flour or chickpea flour
  • 3 green onions, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley and dill, chopped
  • 1 tsp each: cumin, paprika
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 425°F. Line baking sheet with parchment.
  2. 2Mix all ingredients in a bowl until combined.
  3. 3Form into patties and place on sheet. Brush tops with olive oil.
  4. 4Bake 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crispy. Serve with Greek yogurt dip.
