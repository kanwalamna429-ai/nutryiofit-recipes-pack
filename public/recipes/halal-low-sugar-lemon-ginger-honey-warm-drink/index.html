Low Sugar Lemon Ginger Honey Warm Drink — a healthy halal beverage with 90 calories and 2g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups water
  • Juice of 1 large lemon
  • 1-inch fresh ginger, grated
  • 1-2 tbsp raw honey
  • Optional: pinch of turmeric
Instructions
  1. 1Bring water and grated ginger to a gentle simmer for 5 minutes.
  2. 2Remove from heat, add lemon juice and honey. Stir well.
  3. 3Strain and serve warm.
