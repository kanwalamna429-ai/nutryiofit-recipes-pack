Thyroid Friendly Shrimp Salad — a healthy halal recipe with 320 kcal calories and 28g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs halal large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 4 cups mixed greens
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • ½ red onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • Salt, black pepper, and fresh parsley
  • Lemon wedges
Instructions
  1. 1Toss shrimp with 1 tbsp olive oil, garlic, salt, and pepper. Sauté or grill 2-3 minutes per side until pink.
  2. 2In a large bowl, combine greens, avocado, cucumber, and onion.
  3. 3Top with warm shrimp. Drizzle with remaining olive oil and lemon juice. Garnish with parsley.
