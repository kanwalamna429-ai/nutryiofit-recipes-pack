Paneer Saag Curry (with Fresh Spinach) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 320 calories and 17g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 14 oz paneer, cubed
  • 1 lb fresh spinach (or 2 bunches), washed
  • 1 onion, 2 tomatoes, 1-inch ginger, 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin, turmeric, garam masala, salt
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee, ¼ cup cream or coconut milk (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Blanch spinach in boiling water for 1 minute, then blend into a smooth puree.
  2. 2Sauté onion, ginger, garlic, and tomatoes with spices until thick.
  3. 3Add spinach puree and simmer 5 minutes. Gently add fried or raw paneer cubes and simmer 5-7 minutes. Finish with cream if desired.
