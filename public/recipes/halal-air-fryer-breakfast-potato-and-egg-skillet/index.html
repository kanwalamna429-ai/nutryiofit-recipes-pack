Air Fryer Breakfast Potato and Egg Skillet — a healthy halal recipe with 320 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 medium potatoes, diced small
  • 4 eggs
  • ½ onion, diced
  • ½ bell pepper, diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp paprika, garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper
  • Fresh parsley
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat air fryer to 400°F (200°C).
  2. 2 Toss potatoes, onion, and pepper with oil and seasonings. Air fry 12-15 minutes, shaking halfway.
  3. 3 Push veggies aside, crack eggs into spaces. Cook 5-7 more minutes until eggs set.
  4. 4 Garnish with parsley and serve.
