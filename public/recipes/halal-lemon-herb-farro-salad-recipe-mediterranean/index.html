Lemon Herb Farro Salad Recipe (Mediterranean) — a healthy halal recipe with 310 calories and 10g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup farro, rinsed
  • 3 cups vegetable broth or water
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ cup roasted red peppers, diced
  • ½ red onion, diced
  • ½ cup halal feta, crumbled
  • ¼ cup each parsley, mint, dill, chopped
  • Dressing: ⅓ cup olive oil, juice & zest of 1 lemon, 1 tbsp apple vinegar, 1 garlic clove minced, 1 tsp oregano, salt & pepper.
Instructions
  1. 1Cook farro in broth ~25-30 min until chewy-tender. Drain excess liquid and cool.
  2. 2Whisk dressing. Mix farro with veggies, herbs, feta. Toss with dressing.
