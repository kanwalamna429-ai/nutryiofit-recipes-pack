Potato and Lentil Soup (Moroccan Style) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 340 calories and 8g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 3 medium potatoes, cubed
  • ¾ cup green or brown lentils
  • 1 onion, 2 carrots, 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 (14 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 5 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, 2 tsp ras el hanout or cumin + paprika + cinnamon
  • Fresh cilantro and lemon
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion, carrot, and garlic. Add spices and tomatoes.
  2. 2Stir in lentils, potatoes, and broth. Simmer 25-30 minutes until tender.
  3. 3Partially blend if desired. Serve with cilantro and lemon.
  4. 4*Nutritional Value (per serving, approx.): 260 calories, 12g protein, 48g carbs, 5g fat, 10g fiber.
