Healthy Carrot Pineapple Detox Juice — a healthy halal recipe with 90 calories and 2g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 carrots, peeled
  • 1 cup pineapple chunks (fresh or frozen)
  • 1-inch ginger (optional)
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • ½ cup water or coconut water
Instructions
  1. 1 Juice carrots and pineapple (or blend and strain).
  2. 2 Mix with lemon juice and water. Serve fresh.
