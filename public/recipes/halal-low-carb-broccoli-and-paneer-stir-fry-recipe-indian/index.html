Low Carb Broccoli and Paneer Stir Fry Recipe (Indian) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 260 calories and 16g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 8-10 oz paneer (halal), cubed
  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves + ½ inch ginger, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp each: turmeric, garam masala, chili flakes
  • 2 tbsp ghee or olive oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Fry paneer cubes in 1 tbsp ghee until golden; remove and set aside.
  2. 2In same pan, add remaining ghee and cumin seeds. Add onion, ginger-garlic; sauté 2 minutes.
  3. 3Add broccoli, spices, and salt. Stir-fry 6-8 minutes until broccoli is tender-crisp.
  4. 4Return paneer, toss gently, and cook 2 more minutes. Garnish with cilantro.
