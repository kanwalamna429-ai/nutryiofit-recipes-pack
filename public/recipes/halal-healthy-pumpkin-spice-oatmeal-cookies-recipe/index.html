Healthy Pumpkin Spice Oatmeal Cookies — a healthy halal recipe with 130 kcal calories and 3g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1½ cups old-fashioned oats
  • ¾ cup oat flour
  • 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • Pinch salt
  • ¾ cup pumpkin puree
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • ¼ cup olive oil or almond butter
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Optional: ⅓ cup raisins or chopped walnuts
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line baking sheet with parchment.
  2. 2Mix dry ingredients (oats, oat flour, spices, baking soda, salt).
  3. 3In another bowl, whisk pumpkin, maple syrup, oil, egg, and vanilla.
  4. 4Combine wet and dry until dough forms. Fold in add-ins.
  5. 5Scoop and slightly flatten cookies. Bake 12-15 minutes until edges are golden. Cool on rack.
