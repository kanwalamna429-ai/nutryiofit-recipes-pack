Healthy French Onion Soup (No Wine) — a healthy halal recipe with 180 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 3 tbsp olive oil or butter (if halal)
  • 6 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 6 cups halal beef broth
  • 1 tsp thyme, bay leaf, salt & pepper
  • Halal cheese (mozzarella or Swiss) and toasted bread for topping (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Cook onions in oil over medium-low heat, stirring often, 30-40 min until deeply caramelized.
  2. 2Add garlic, vinegar, broth, thyme, and bay leaf. Simmer 15-20 min.
  3. 3Remove bay leaf. Ladle into bowls and top with toasted bread and melted cheese if desired.
