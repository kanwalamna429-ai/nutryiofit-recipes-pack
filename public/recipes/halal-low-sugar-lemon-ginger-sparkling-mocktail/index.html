Low Sugar Lemon Ginger Sparkling Mocktail — a healthy halal recipe with 25 calories and 0g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2-inch fresh ginger, grated
  • Juice of 3 lemons
  • 4 cups sparkling water
  • Stevia or monk fruit sweetener to taste
  • Ice cubes
  • Lemon slices and mint for garnish
Instructions
  1. 1 Muddle grated ginger with lemon juice and sweetener in a pitcher.
  2. 2 Let sit 5 minutes to infuse.
  3. 3 Add sparkling water and ice just before serving. Garnish.
