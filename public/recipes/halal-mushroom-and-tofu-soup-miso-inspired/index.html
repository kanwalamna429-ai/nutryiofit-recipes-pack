Mushroom and Tofu Soup (Miso Inspired) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 160 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 8 oz firm tofu, cubed
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 2-3 tbsp white or red miso paste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 scallions, 1-inch ginger, nori strips (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Simmer broth with ginger and mushrooms for 10 minutes.
  2. 2Add tofu and simmer 5 minutes. Remove from heat.
  3. 3Dissolve miso in a little warm broth then stir into soup. Add soy sauce and sesame oil. Garnish with scallions and nori.
