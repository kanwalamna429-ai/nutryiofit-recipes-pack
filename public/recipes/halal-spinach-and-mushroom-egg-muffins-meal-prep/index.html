Spinach and Mushroom Egg Muffins (Meal Prep) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 180 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 8 eggs
  • 2 cups fresh spinach, chopped
  • 8 oz mushrooms, finely chopped
  • ½ cup milk or plant milk
  • ½ cup shredded cheese (optional)
  • 1 garlic clove, salt, pepper, Italian herbs
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté mushrooms until moisture evaporates. Add spinach and garlic until wilted. Cool slightly.
  2. 2Whisk eggs, milk, salt, pepper, and herbs. Stir in mushroom-spinach mixture and cheese.
  3. 3Divide into greased muffin tin. Bake at 375°F for 20-25 minutes until set. Cool and store for meal prep.
