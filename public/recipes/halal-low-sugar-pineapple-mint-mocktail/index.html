Low Sugar Pineapple Mint Mocktail — a healthy halal recipe with 65 calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1½ cups fresh pineapple chunks
  • 10 fresh mint leaves
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 1 cup sparkling water
  • 1 tsp monk fruit sweetener (optional)
  • Ice cubes
Instructions
  1. 1 Blend pineapple, mint, lime juice, and sweetener with a splash of water until smooth.
  2. 2 Strain for clearer texture if preferred.
  3. 3 Divide between glasses with ice and top with sparkling water. Stir gently.
