Low Carb Mushroom Onion Breakfast Quiche — a healthy halal recipe with 230 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 8 eggs
  • 2 cups sliced mushrooms
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup shredded cheese (mozzarella or Swiss)
  • ½ cup heavy cream or milk
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, thyme
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease pie dish.
  2. 2 Sauté mushrooms and onions in oil until golden. Spread in dish.
  3. 3 Whisk eggs, cream, salt, pepper, and thyme. Stir in half the cheese.
  4. 4 Pour over veggies, top with remaining cheese. Bake 30-35 minutes.
