Meal Prep Roasted Veggie and Quinoa Jar Salad — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 360 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup quinoa, cooked
  • 4 cups mixed roasted vegetables (eggplant, zucchini, carrots, peppers)
  • 1 cup chickpeas or white beans
  • Baby greens
  • Lemon-olive oil dressing, feta or olives (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Roast chopped vegetables with olive oil, salt, and herbs at 425°F for 20-25 minutes. Cool.
  2. 2Layer in jars: dressing at bottom, then roasted veggies, beans, quinoa, and greens on top.
  3. 3Seal and refrigerate up to 5 days. Shake to mix when ready to eat.
