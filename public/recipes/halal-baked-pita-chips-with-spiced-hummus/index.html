Baked Pita Chips with Spiced Hummus — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 320 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • Chips: 4-5 whole wheat pita breads, cut into triangles, olive oil, za'atar or sumac, salt
Instructions
  1. 1Toss pita triangles with oil, za'atar, and salt. Bake at 400°F (205°C) for 8-12 minutes until crispy.
  2. 2Blend hummus ingredients until smooth, adding water as needed.
  3. 3Serve chips with hummus drizzled with olive oil and spices. Perfect for snacking or mezze.
