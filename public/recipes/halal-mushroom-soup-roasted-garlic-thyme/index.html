Mushroom Soup (Roasted Garlic Thyme) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 160 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 12 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 whole garlic bulb, roasted
  • 1 onion, 2 celery stalks
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 cup milk or coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, fresh thyme, salt, pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Roast garlic at 400°F for 25 minutes. Sauté onion, celery, and mushrooms until browned.
  2. 2Squeeze roasted garlic into pot. Add broth and thyme; simmer 15 minutes.
  3. 3Blend partially or fully. Stir in milk and heat through. Adjust seasoning.
