Indian Style Zucchini Curry - Light Summer — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 120 calories and 10g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 3-4 zucchinis, sliced or cubed
  • 1 onion, tomato
  • Cumin, turmeric, coriander, mild chili/garam masala
  • Ginger, optional yogurt or coconut milk for lightness
  • Oil, salt, cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Lightly sauté cumin, onion, ginger, tomato masala.
  2. 2Add zucchini; cook covered until tender but not mushy (quick). Light, refreshing curry.
