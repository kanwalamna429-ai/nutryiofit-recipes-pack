Creamy Banana Date Smoothie — a healthy halal beverage with 380 calories and 10g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 frozen bananas
  • 4-5 pitted Medjool dates
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of cinnamon
Instructions
  1. 1Add all ingredients to a blender.
  2. 2Blend until thick, creamy, and naturally sweet.
  3. 3Serve as a satisfying energy-boosting smoothie.
