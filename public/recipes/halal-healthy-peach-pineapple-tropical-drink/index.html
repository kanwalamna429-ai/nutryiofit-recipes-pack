Healthy Peach Pineapple Tropical Drink — a healthy halal recipe with 200 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup frozen peaches
  • 1 cup frozen pineapple chunks
  • 1 cup coconut water
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • ½ cup unsweetened almond milk
  • Ice cubes
Instructions
  1. 1 Blend peaches, pineapple, coconut water, and lime juice.
  2. 2 Add almond milk and blend until smooth.
  3. 3 Serve over ice.
