Homemade Lemon Basil Sparkling Water — a healthy halal recipe with 20 calories and 0g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • Juice of 3 lemons
  • 12–15 fresh basil leaves
  • 4 cups sparkling water
  • Stevia to taste (optional)
  • Ice cubes
  • Lemon slices and basil for garnish
Instructions
  1. 1 Muddle basil with lemon juice and sweetener in a pitcher.
  2. 2 Add sparkling water and ice just before serving.
  3. 3 Garnish and enjoy.
