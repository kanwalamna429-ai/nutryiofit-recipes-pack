Gluten Free Almond Butter Banana Breakfast Cookies — a healthy halal recipe with 320 calories and 12g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1 cup almond butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup rolled oats (gluten-free)
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ¼ cup dark chocolate chips (halal, optional)
  • Pinch of salt
Instructions
  1. 1 Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line baking sheet.
  2. 2 Mix all ingredients until combined.
  3. 3 Scoop onto sheet and flatten slightly.
  4. 4 Bake 12-15 minutes until golden. Cool on rack.
