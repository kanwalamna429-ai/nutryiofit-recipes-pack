Vegetarian Freekeh with Peas and Carrots — a healthy halal recipe with 290 calories and 10g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup freekeh, rinsed
  • 2 carrots, diced; 1 cup green peas
  • 1 onion, diced; 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp allspice, salt & pepper
  • 2¼ cups vegetable broth
  • Fresh parsley and mint
Instructions
  1. 1Heat oil and sauté onion 4 min. Add garlic, carrots, and spices; cook 2 min.
  2. 2Add freekeh and stir 1 min. Pour in broth and bring to boil.
  3. 3Reduce heat, add peas, cover and simmer 20-25 min until freekeh is tender.
  4. 4Fluff and garnish with fresh herbs.
