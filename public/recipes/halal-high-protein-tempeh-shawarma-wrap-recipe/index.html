High Protein Tempeh Shawarma Wrap Recipe — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 420 calories and 22g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 8-10 oz tempeh, thinly sliced
  • Shawarma spices: 2 tsp each cumin, paprika; 1 tsp turmeric, garlic powder, cinnamon; salt, pepper
  • 3 tbsp olive oil + 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • Veggies: tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, lettuce
  • Tahini sauce or garlic yogurt
  • Whole wheat or gluten-free wraps
Instructions
  1. 1Marinate tempeh in spices, oil, and lemon for 30+ min.
  2. 2Pan-sear or bake at 400°F until crispy (10-15 min).
  3. 3Assemble wraps with veggies and sauce.
