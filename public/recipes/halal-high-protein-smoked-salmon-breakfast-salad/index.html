High Protein Smoked Salmon Breakfast Salad — a healthy halal recipe with 380 calories and 28g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 oz halal smoked salmon (or lox)
  • 4 cups mixed greens
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs, halved
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • Lemon vinaigrette (lemon juice, olive oil, dill)
Instructions
  1. 1 Arrange greens in bowls. Top with flaked salmon, egg halves, avocado, cucumber, onion, and capers.
  2. 2 Drizzle with lemon-olive oil dressing and fresh dill.
  3. 3 Season with black pepper.
