Air Fryer Baked Potato (Stuffed Broccoli Cheddar) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 380 calories and 18g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 medium russet potatoes
  • 2 cups broccoli florets, chopped and steamed
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • ½ cup Greek yogurt or sour cream
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • Salt, pepper, olive oil spray
Instructions
  1. 1Pierce potatoes, spray lightly with oil, and air fry at 400°F (205°C) for 35-45 minutes until tender.
  2. 2Scoop out some flesh, mix with broccoli, half the cheese, yogurt, salt, and pepper.
  3. 3Refill potatoes, top with remaining cheese. Air fry 5-8 more minutes until cheese melts. Garnish with green onions.
