Pear Walnut Oatmeal — a healthy halal breakfast with 310 calories and 10g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • ½ cup oats
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 diced pear
  • 1 tbsp walnuts
  • 1 tsp honey
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
Instructions
  1. 1Cook oats with milk and pear 5 min.
  2. 2Top with walnuts, honey, cinnamon.
