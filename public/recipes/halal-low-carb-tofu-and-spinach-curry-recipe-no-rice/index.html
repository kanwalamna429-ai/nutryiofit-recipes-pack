Low Carb Tofu and Spinach Curry Recipe (No Rice) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 240 calories and 16g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 14 oz extra-firm tofu, cubed and pressed
  • 8-10 oz fresh spinach
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves + 1 inch ginger
  • 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 1 tsp each: cumin, turmeric, garam masala
  • ½ tsp chili powder
  • 3 tbsp olive oil or coconut oil
  • ½ cup coconut milk (optional for creaminess)
  • Salt and fresh cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Pan-fry tofu cubes until golden; set aside.
  2. 2Sauté onion until golden. Add ginger-garlic and spices. Cook 1 minute.
  3. 3Add tomatoes and simmer 8 minutes. Stir in spinach until wilted.
  4. 4Return tofu, add coconut milk if using, and simmer 5 minutes. Garnish with cilantro.
