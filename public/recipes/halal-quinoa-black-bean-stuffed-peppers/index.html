Quinoa Black Bean Stuffed Peppers — a healthy halal recipe with 340 calories and 14g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 large bell peppers, halved and seeded
  • 1 cup quinoa, cooked
  • 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained
  • 1 cup corn kernels, 1 tomato, 1 red onion (diced)
  • 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp chili powder, ½ tsp smoked paprika, salt & pepper
  • 1 cup salsa, fresh cilantro, lime wedges, optional cheese
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven 400°F. Mix cooked quinoa, black beans, corn, tomato, onion, spices, and half the salsa.
  2. 2Stuff pepper halves generously. Place in baking dish.
  3. 3Top with remaining salsa. Cover and bake 30-35 min until peppers are tender.
  4. 4Garnish with cilantro and lime.
