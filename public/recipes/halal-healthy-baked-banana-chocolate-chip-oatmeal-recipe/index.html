Healthy Baked Banana Chocolate Chip Oatmeal — a healthy halal recipe with 280 kcal calories and 9g protein per serving.

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup maple syrup
  • ½ cup dark chocolate chips (sugar-controlled)
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
