Air Fryer Cauliflower Wings (Spicy Garlic) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 160 calories and 6g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 large head cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 1/2 cup almond flour or cornstarch
  • 1 tsp garlic powder, smoked paprika
  • 1/2 cup buffalo or hot sauce
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup or honey
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Ranch dressing for serving
Instructions
  1. 1Toss cauliflower with flour and dry spices.
  2. 2Air fry at 400°F (205°C) for 15 minutes until crispy.
  3. 3Mix hot sauce, sweetener, and oil. Toss florets in sauce and air fry 5 more minutes. Serve with ranch.
