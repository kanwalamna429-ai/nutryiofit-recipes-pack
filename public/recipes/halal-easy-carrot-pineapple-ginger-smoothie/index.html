Easy Carrot Pineapple Ginger Smoothie — a healthy halal recipe with 145 calories and 2g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 medium carrots, peeled and chopped
  • 1½ cups frozen pineapple chunks
  • 1-inch fresh ginger, peeled
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 cup unsweetened coconut water
  • Ice as needed
Instructions
  1. 1 Place carrots, pineapple, ginger, banana, and coconut water in a high-speed blender.
  2. 2 Blend until completely smooth, adding ice for desired thickness.
  3. 3 Pour into glasses and serve fresh.
