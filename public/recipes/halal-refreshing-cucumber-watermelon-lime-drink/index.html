Refreshing Cucumber Watermelon Lime Drink — a healthy halal recipe with 55 calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 3 cups watermelon chunks
  • 1 large cucumber, peeled
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 2 cups cold sparkling water
  • Handful fresh mint leaves
  • Ice cubes
Instructions
  1. 1 Blend watermelon and cucumber until smooth. Strain if desired.
  2. 2 Stir in lime juice and mint.
  3. 3 Add sparkling water just before serving over ice.
