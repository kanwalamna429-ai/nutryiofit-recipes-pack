Dairy Free Coconut Chicken Soup — a healthy halal recipe with 340 kcal calories and 26g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs halal chicken thighs, sliced thin
  • 1 (13.5 oz) can full-fat coconut milk
  • 4 cups halal chicken broth
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (zucchini, carrots, spinach)
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Fresh cilantro
Instructions
  1. 1Heat coconut oil in a pot. Sauté onion, garlic, and ginger for 3 minutes. Add chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  2. 2Pour in broth and coconut milk. Bring to simmer.
  3. 3Add vegetables and cook 15-20 minutes until chicken is tender. Stir in lime juice. Garnish with cilantro.
