Healthy Tahini Roasted Vegetable Bowl — a healthy halal recipe with 480 calories and 16g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cauliflower, 2 sweet potatoes, 2 carrots, 1 red onion (all cubed)
  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, 2 tsp za’atar or cumin + paprika, salt & pepper
  • Tahini sauce: ¼ cup tahini, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 1 garlic clove, water to thin, salt
  • Fresh parsley, quinoa or rice base
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven 425°F. Toss vegetables and chickpeas with oil and spices on two baking sheets.
  2. 2Roast 30-35 min, tossing halfway until caramelized.
  3. 3Whisk tahini sauce ingredients, adding water until pourable.
  4. 4Serve over quinoa with roasted veggies and generous tahini drizzle. Garnish with parsley.
