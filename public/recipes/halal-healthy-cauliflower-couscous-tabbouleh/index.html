Healthy Cauliflower Couscous Tabbouleh — a healthy halal recipe with 220 calories and 8g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 large cauliflower, riced (about 5-6 cups)
  • 2 cups fresh parsley, ½ cup mint, finely chopped
  • 4 green onions, 3-4 tomatoes, diced
  • ¼ cup olive oil, juice of 2 lemons
  • 1 tsp cumin, salt & pepper
  • Optional: 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas
Instructions
  1. 1Pulse cauliflower in food processor to rice consistency or use pre-riced.
  2. 2Sauté or steam cauliflower rice 5-7 min until just tender. Cool completely.
  3. 3Mix with chopped herbs, green onions, tomatoes, and chickpeas if using.
  4. 4Whisk oil, lemon, cumin, salt, and pepper. Toss with tabbouleh. Adjust seasoning.
