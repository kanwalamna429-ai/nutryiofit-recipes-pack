Easy Caprese Style Breakfast Sandwich — a healthy halal recipe with 420 calories and 24g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 whole grain English muffins or gluten-free, toasted
  • 4 eggs, fried or scrambled
  • 4 slices fresh mozzarella
  • 1 large tomato, sliced
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp balsamic glaze
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional: arugula
Instructions
  1. 1 Cook eggs seasoned with salt and pepper.
  2. 2 Layer on toasted muffins: mozzarella, tomato, basil, eggs, more cheese if desired.
  3. 3 Drizzle with balsamic glaze. Serve immediately.
