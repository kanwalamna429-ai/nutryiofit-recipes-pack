Low Carb Blueberry Almond Bread — a healthy halal recipe with 220 kcal calories and 8g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups almond flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp cinnamon, pinch salt
  • 3 eggs
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup or monk fruit sweetener
  • ¼ cup olive oil or melted coconut oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup fresh or frozen blueberries
  • Optional: ⅓ cup sliced almonds
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease loaf pan.
  2. 2Whisk almond flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt.
  3. 3Mix eggs, sweetener, oil, and vanilla.
  4. 4Combine wet and dry. Gently fold in blueberries (and almonds).
  5. 5Bake 45-50 minutes until set. Cool completely.
