Low Calorie Mediterranean Breakfast Flatbread — a healthy halal recipe with 280 calories and 18g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 whole wheat or low-carb flatbreads/pitas (halal)
  • 4 egg whites or 2 whole eggs, scrambled
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced
  • 1/4 cup feta cheese, crumbled
  • 1/4 cup olives, sliced
  • Fresh herbs (mint, parsley), za’atar
  • 2 tbsp Greek yogurt
  • Olive oil drizzle
Instructions
  1. 1 Warm flatbreads. Scramble eggs.
  2. 2 Spread yogurt on flatbreads, top with eggs, veggies, feta, olives, and herbs.
  3. 3 Sprinkle za’atar and light olive oil. Fold or serve open-faced.
