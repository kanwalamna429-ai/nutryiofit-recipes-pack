Healthy Bulgur Pilaf Side Dish Recipe — a healthy halal recipe with 180 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup coarse bulgur wheat
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp cumin, salt & pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion and carrot in olive oil until softened (5 min).
  2. 2Add bulgur, tomato, cumin, salt & pepper. Stir 1-2 min.
  3. 3Pour in broth, bring to boil, then simmer covered 15-20 min until liquid is absorbed. Fluff and stir in parsley. Serve warm.
