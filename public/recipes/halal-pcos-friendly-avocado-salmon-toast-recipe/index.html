PCOS Friendly Avocado Salmon Toast — a healthy halal recipe with 420 kcal calories and 32g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 halal salmon fillets (5-6 oz each) or canned salmon
  • 2 ripe avocados, mashed
  • 4 slices gluten-free or low-carb bread (or lettuce wraps for lower carb)
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp everything bagel seasoning or salt + pepper
  • Cherry tomatoes and microgreens (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Pan-sear or bake salmon until cooked. Flake or slice.
  2. 2Toast bread if using. Spread mashed avocado mixed with lemon juice.
  3. 3Top with salmon, seasoning, tomatoes, and greens.
