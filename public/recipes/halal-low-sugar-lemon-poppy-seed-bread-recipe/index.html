Low Sugar Lemon Poppy Seed Bread — a healthy halal recipe with 160 kcal calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1½ cups white whole wheat flour
  • 1 tbsp poppy seeds
  • 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp baking soda
  • Pinch salt
  • Zest of 2 lemons + 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 eggs
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp vanilla
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease loaf pan.
  2. 2Whisk flour, poppy seeds, baking powder, soda, and salt.
  3. 3Mix lemon zest/juice, eggs, maple, oil, yogurt, and vanilla.
  4. 4Combine wet and dry until smooth.
  5. 5Bake 45-55 minutes. Cool before slicing.
