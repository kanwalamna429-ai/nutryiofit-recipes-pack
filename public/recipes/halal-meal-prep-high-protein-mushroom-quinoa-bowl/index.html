Meal Prep High Protein Mushroom Quinoa Bowl — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 420 calories and 20g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 ½ cups quinoa, cooked
  • 1 lb mushrooms, sliced
  • 8 oz spinach or kale
  • 1 cup chickpeas
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ cup feta or goat cheese
  • Thyme, olive oil, lemon juice, salt, pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté mushrooms until golden. Add garlic, chickpeas, and greens until wilted.
  2. 2Season with thyme, lemon, salt, and pepper.
  3. 3Layer quinoa and mushroom mixture in containers. Top with cheese. Store up to 5 days. Reheat or eat cold.
