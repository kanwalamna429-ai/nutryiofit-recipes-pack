High Protein Turkey Zucchini Meatballs — a healthy halal recipe with 310 kcal calories and 35g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbs halal ground turkey
  • 2 medium zucchinis, grated and squeezed dry
  • 1 egg
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano and basil
  • 1 tsp salt and black pepper
  • Olive oil for baking
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 400°F. Mix all ingredients thoroughly.
  2. 2Form into meatballs and place on lined baking sheet.
  3. 3Bake 20-25 minutes until cooked through. Serve with zucchini noodles or salad.
