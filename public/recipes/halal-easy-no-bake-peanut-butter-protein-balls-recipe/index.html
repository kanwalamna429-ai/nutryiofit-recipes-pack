Easy No-Bake Peanut Butter Protein Balls — a healthy halal recipe with 280 kcal calories and 15g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup rolled oats (gluten-free if needed)
  • 1/2 cup natural peanut butter (no added sugar)
  • 2 scoops vanilla or unflavored whey/plant protein powder (~40g protein total)
  • 2 Tbsp ground flaxseed
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2-3 Tbsp unsweetened almond milk (as needed)
  • Optional: 1-2 Tbsp sugar-free chocolate chips or chopped peanuts
Instructions
  1. 1Mix oats, protein powder, and flaxseed in a bowl.
  2. 2Add peanut butter, vanilla, and milk; stir until a dough forms.
  3. 3Roll into 12 balls. Chill 30 minutes to firm up. Store in fridge up to 1 week.
