Iced Lavender Chamomile Milk Drink — a healthy halal recipe with 85 calories and 1g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 chamomile tea bags
  • 1 tsp food-grade dried lavender buds
  • 2 cups unsweetened almond milk
  • 1–2 tsp honey or maple syrup
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • Ice cubes
  • Lavender or chamomile flowers for garnish
Instructions
  1. 1 Heat almond milk and steep chamomile tea bags with lavender for 5–7 minutes.
  2. 2 Remove tea bags and buds. Stir in honey and vanilla.
  3. 3 Cool completely, then pour over ice and garnish.
