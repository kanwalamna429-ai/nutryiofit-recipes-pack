Roasted Eggplant Tomato Soup — a healthy halal recipe with 160 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 medium eggplants, cubed
  • 2 lbs tomatoes, halved
  • 1 onion, quartered; 5 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano, salt & pepper
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • Fresh basil
Instructions
  1. 1Toss eggplant, tomatoes, onion, garlic with oil, oregano, salt, and pepper. Roast at 425°F for 40 min.
  2. 2Blend roasted vegetables with broth until smooth.
  3. 3Simmer 10-15 min. Garnish with fresh basil.
