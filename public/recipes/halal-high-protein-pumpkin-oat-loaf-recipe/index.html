High Protein Pumpkin Oat Loaf — a healthy halal recipe with 165 kcal calories and 10g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup pumpkin puree
  • 1½ cups oat flour
  • ½ cup vanilla protein powder (halal)
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • Pinch salt
  • 3 eggs
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • ½ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease loaf pan.
  2. 2Whisk oat flour, protein powder, baking soda, spice, and salt.
  3. 3Mix pumpkin, eggs, maple, yogurt, oil, and vanilla.
  4. 4Combine wet and dry until smooth.
  5. 5Bake 50-60 minutes. Cool fully before slicing.
