Vegetarian Harira Soup (Moroccan Chickpeas Lentils) — a healthy halal recipe with 240 calories and 13g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • ¾ cup green lentils, rinsed
  • 1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained
  • 1 large onion, 2 celery stalks, 2 carrots (diced)
  • 4 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 tsp ras el hanout or (cumin + turmeric + cinnamon + paprika)
  • 1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes + 6 cups veg broth
  • ¼ cup chopped cilantro & parsley, lemon wedges
  • Optional: 2 tbsp rice or vermicelli
Instructions
  1. 1Sauté onion, celery, carrots 5-7 min. Add garlic, ginger, spices.
  2. 2Add tomatoes, lentils, broth. Simmer 30-35 min until lentils tender.
  3. 3Add chickpeas and pasta (if using); cook 8-10 min more. Stir in herbs. Serve with lemon.
