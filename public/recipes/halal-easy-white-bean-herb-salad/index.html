Easy White Bean Herb Salad — a healthy halal recipe with 220 calories and 11g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 (15-oz) cans white beans, drained
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ½ red onion, diced
  • ¼ cup fresh basil, parsley, and oregano, chopped
  • Dressing: ¼ cup olive oil, juice of 1-2 lemons, 2 garlic cloves minced, salt & pepper.
Instructions
  1. 1Whisk dressing.
  2. 2Combine beans, tomatoes, onion, and herbs.
  3. 3Pour dressing over and toss. Let marinate 10 min.
