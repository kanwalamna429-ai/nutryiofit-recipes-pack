High Protein Smoked Salmon Breakfast Stack — a healthy halal recipe with 450 calories and 32g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 4 oz halal smoked salmon
  • 4 large eggs, poached or fried
  • 2 whole grain English muffins or gluten-free, toasted
  • 1 avocado, mashed
  • 1 cup arugula or spinach
  • 2 tbsp Greek yogurt mixed with dill and lemon
  • Capers, black pepper, lemon wedges
Instructions
  1. 1 Toast muffins. Spread with mashed avocado.
  2. 2 Layer arugula, smoked salmon, and eggs.
  3. 3 Drizzle with herbed yogurt. Top with capers and pepper.
  4. 4 Serve immediately with lemon wedges.
