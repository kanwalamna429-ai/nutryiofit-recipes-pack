Homemade Beet Orange Carrot Juice — a healthy halal recipe with 150 calories and 3g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 medium beets, peeled
  • 3 oranges, peeled
  • 3 medium carrots
  • 1-inch fresh ginger (optional)
  • Juice of ½ lemon
Instructions
  1. 1 Juice beets, oranges, and carrots in a juicer.
  2. 2 Add ginger if using.
  3. 3 Stir in lemon juice and serve fresh.
