Homemade Blueberry Mint Mocktail — a healthy halal recipe with 65 calories and 5g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup fresh or frozen blueberries
  • 10–12 fresh mint leaves
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 2 cups sparkling water
  • 1–2 tbsp honey or stevia (optional)
  • Ice cubes
Instructions
  1. 1 Muddle blueberries and mint with lime juice.
  2. 2 Strain into pitcher if smoother texture preferred.
  3. 3 Add sparkling water and ice. Stir gently and serve.
