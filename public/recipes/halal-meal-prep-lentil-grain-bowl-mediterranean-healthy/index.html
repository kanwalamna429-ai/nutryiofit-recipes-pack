Meal Prep Lentil Grain Bowl (Mediterranean Healthy) — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 420 calories and 18g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup green lentils, cooked
  • 2 cups cooked quinoa or farro
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup kalamata olives
  • ⅓ cup olive oil + lemon juice dressing
  • Fresh parsley, mint, feta (optional), salt, pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Cook lentils and grain according to package. Cool slightly.
  2. 2Toss everything with lemon-olive oil dressing, salt, and pepper.
  3. 3Divide into 4 containers. Keeps in fridge up to 5 days. Add fresh greens when serving.
