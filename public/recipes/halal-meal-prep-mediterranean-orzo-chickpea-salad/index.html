Meal Prep Mediterranean Orzo Chickpea Salad — a healthy halal vegetarian recipe with 460 calories and 18g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 ½ cups orzo pasta, cooked and cooled
  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas
  • 1 cucumber, 2 tomatoes, 1 bell pepper, diced
  • ½ red onion, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup kalamata olives
  • ⅓ cup olive oil + red wine vinegar dressing
  • Fresh oregano, parsley, feta (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1Cook orzo, drain, and rinse under cold water.
  2. 2Toss with chickpeas, vegetables, olives, herbs, and dressing. Season well.
  3. 3Divide into containers. Keeps in fridge 4-5 days. Stir before eating.
