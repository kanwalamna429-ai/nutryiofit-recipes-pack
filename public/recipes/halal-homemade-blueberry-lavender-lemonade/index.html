Homemade Blueberry Lavender Lemonade — a healthy halal beverage with 90 calories and 2g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup fresh or frozen blueberries
  • Juice of 4-5 large lemons (about ¾ cup)
  • 4-5 cups cold water
  • 2-3 tbsp honey or maple syrup (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tsp food-grade dried lavender buds
  • Ice and fresh blueberries + lemon slices for garnish
Instructions
  1. 1Gently heat 1 cup water with lavender and honey for 3-5 minutes (do not boil). Strain and cool.
  2. 2Blend blueberries with lemon juice until smooth, then strain if desired.
  3. 3Combine blueberry-lemon mixture with lavender water and remaining cold water. Stir, chill, and serve over ice.
