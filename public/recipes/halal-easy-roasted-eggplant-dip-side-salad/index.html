Easy Roasted Eggplant Dip Side Salad — a healthy halal recipe with 110 calories and 3g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 large eggplants
  • 2 tbsp tahini
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • Juice of 1-2 lemons
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • Olive oil for drizzling
  • Salt & pepper
Instructions
  1. 1Preheat oven 425°F. Pierce eggplants and roast 35-40 min until collapsed. Cool, scoop flesh.
  2. 2Mash eggplant with tahini, garlic, lemon juice, salt & pepper.
  3. 3Fold in tomatoes and parsley. Drizzle with olive oil. Serve as dip or salad with pita.
