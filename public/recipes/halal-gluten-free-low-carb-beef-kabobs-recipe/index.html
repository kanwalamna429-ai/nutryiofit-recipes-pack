Gluten Free Low Carb Beef Kabobs — a healthy halal recipe with 380 kcal calories and 38g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 lbs halal beef sirloin, cut into chunks
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tsp dried rosemary and thyme
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Bell peppers and red onion chunks (for skewering)
  • Wooden skewers (soaked)
Instructions
  1. 1Mix oil, garlic, lemon, herbs, paprika, salt, and pepper. Marinate beef 30 minutes.
  2. 2Thread beef, peppers, and onion alternately onto skewers.
  3. 3Grill or broil 5-6 minutes per side until beef reaches desired doneness.
