Indian Style Chana Masala (Street Style) — a healthy vegetarian halal recipe with 240 calories and 11g protein per serving.

Ingredients
  • 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained
  • 1 large onion, 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp turmeric, 2 tsp chana masala powder, ½ tsp chili powder
  • 2 tbsp oil, fresh cilantro, lemon wedges
Instructions
  1. 1Heat oil, add cumin seeds. Sauté onion until golden. Add ginger-garlic and tomato puree.
  2. 2Cook spices 5–6 min until oil separates.
  3. 3Add chickpeas and ½ cup water. Simmer 10–12 min. Mash a few chickpeas for thickness.
  4. 4Garnish with cilantro and lemon. Serve with bhature or rice.
